Shin bai isheki abun alfahari ba, yoo idan kika yi tafiya na kwana 2 ko ma na fiye da haka, mijinki yasan ce wa tabbas kina da matukar muhimmanci a garesa shin bai ishe ki abun alfahari ba, mata mu kasance masu tsafta akoda yaushe, ai idan muka yi duba ga Harira za ku ga cewa koda ta bata wa Ahmad rai, sam baya fushi da ita da zaran ya shake dadadan ƙamshin turaren ta kasancewar kaf a cikin matansa, ita ce kadai mai tsafta ita ce mai saka turare akoda yaushe, duk da cewa ba kwarariya ce ba, a fannin kwanciyar aure haka ma a fanni girke-girke ma ita din ba gwana ce ba, amma sa bo da tsaftanta sam Ya Ahmad baya fushi da ita, to don Allah mu yi koyi da ita, haka ma ina son ko wace mace ta yi koyi da halin Hadiza.


Ai idan kuka duba zaku ga Hadiza fa akwai iya tarairayar mai house uwa uba ga iya sarrafa girki iri DABAN-DABAN, ina son mu sani wallahi duk macen da ta iya girki to fa ki sani, gida naki ne, ke fa ba maganar komai idan ciki bai koshi ba, Yar'uwa ki kasance gwana wajen iya sarrafa girki, ki iya girki kala-kala, ai yanzu komai ya zama na social media idan ma kina jin kunyar ki tambaye wata kawar yanda ake abu, to don Allah ki nema a social media mana, ai bazaki rasa wace zata koya miki ba, mata mu tashi mu kasance akoda yaushe girkin mu shi ne abu mafi soyuwa ga mazajen mu, ki kasance akoda yaushe idan ba girkin ki miji yaci ba, to zai ji kamar ma bai koshi ba, shi dai har sai ya ci naki, haka kuma don Allah mata mu kasance masu faranta ran mazajen mu idan aka zo fannin kwanciyar aure, haba sai ka ce ba mace ba, ki san gun da zaki taba a jikin mijin ki yaji ba abunda yake bukata a wannan lokacin face ke, kamata ace muyi koyi da halin Hafiza ai idan kuka lura sam Ya Ahmad baya son abunda zai taba masa ita, sam baya son ta yi ciwo, kasancewar ita ce mai jiyarda shi dadi a fannin kwanciyar aure, shin me yasa ke ma bazaki kasance mace mai faranta ran mijinta ba akoda yaushe, mu kasance masu biyyaya wa mazajenmu, mu kasance masu haƙuri da kuma juriya, Ubangiji Allah ya kara hadan kanmu da mazanmu ya kuma barmu tare dasu, ya kore shaidan a tsakaninmu.
