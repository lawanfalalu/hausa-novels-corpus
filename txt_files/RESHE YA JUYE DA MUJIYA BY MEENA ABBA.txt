Kara shiru masallacin yayi sai malam usman da ake kallo.kara maimaitawa liman yayi, wani daga cikin matasan garin shidai yana son khadija tuntuni tayimashi amma yakasa ya furta mata amma yau tunda ga dama tasamu bari yabari yayi shawara da mahaifinshi komi kenan sai aje agayama malam usman. 



Malamin islamiyarsu khadija ma yana nan kamar zaiyi magana saiya fasa yace bari yabari yaje yaji ta bakinta sannan komai kenan seya fadama mahaifin nata. Kowa da tunanin da yakeyi cikin ranshi liman ne yace to duk kunyi shiru nasan akwai kunya dazata iya hana wasu, wasu kuma sai sunyi shawara da manyansu dan haka idan kuntashi ku samu malam usman baban yarinyar ma assalam. Liman yakoma zai zauna sukaji wani yace *NI INA SON TA KUMA ZAN AURE TA* gaba daya masallacin juyowa akayi dan ganin waye amma abun mamaki wata bakuwar fuska suka gani wanda daga gani ba dan garin bane sanye yake da kaya daban daban daka ganshi kasan a wahalce ce yake. 



Liman ne yace alhamdulillah saika mike dan muga wanene kai kuma dan wane gida ne mikewa yayi kallo gaba daya yakoma kanshi liman mamaki ne yacikashi ganin baitaba ganin fuskarshi ba. Sunkuyowa liman yayi yace malam usman kaganshi gaskia bansanshi a garin nan ba. Malam baiba liman amsaba kawai yace yaro nabaka auren diyata dan haka kasameni gidana.malam usman bakasan ko waye ba zaka yanke hukunci nabashi koma waye ai diya tace nike da hakkin zabar mata miji saboda haka na bashi ba ruwana da ko waye shi.
