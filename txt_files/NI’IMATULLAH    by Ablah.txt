SHAFI NA ASHIRIN DA BIYAR 25

Da sauri ya k’araso daman tunda yayi parking d’in motar sa yake hango abin da yake faruwa,yana k’arasowa ya farayiwa NI’IMATULLAH magan duk da cewa baisan ko wacece ba kuma baisan sunan taba,baiwar Allah baiwar Allah kawai yake fad’a amma ina sam bata cikin hayyacinta,abinka da babban gida duk mutanen gidan basusan abin da ake ba,dan momy tana d’akin ta Ita kuma inaya ma bata nan,mai gadi ne shima ya tawo da gudu yana kallon inda NU’AIMA tayi kwance tayi plate,NA’IM hankalinsa ne ya rabu gida biyu wajen wa zaije wajen NU’AIMA dake kwance daya hango fad’uwarta koko wajen paris?”
Ganin paris yana alamun neman taimako sosai dan yafara fita a hayyacinsa yasashi sauri k’arasawa tabbas wanan lamarin ba wanan yarinyar bace ke yi,ya fahimci duk abin da ke faruwa aikuwa da sauri ya d’aura hannunsa bisa kanta ya rufe idonsa  sosai yafara karanto wasu addu’o’i.”
Ai data saki paris yayi taga taga yayi baya da k’arfi ya fad’i a k’asa sai tafara rawa jikin ta yana rawa yana kakkarwa,tana ta wani numfashi sama sama can sai ta saki k’ara ta zube a k’asa duk da k’arar datayi wanan bai hana NA’IM cigaba da abin da yake ba wani gurnani tafara yi tamkar wata zakanya...


Momy ce ta jiyo wanan k’ara sai ta lek’o ta window na sama aikuwa ta hango duk abubuwan da suke faruwa da sauri jikin ta na rawa take sakkow a kuma daidai wanan lokacin INAYATULLAH tadawo daga inda taje....


Paris ne ya tashi dakyar ya koma kan NU’AIMA yana girgizaata amma ina ko motsi batayi,ya kallo d’ayan b’angaren daake gunurzu da NI’IMATULLAH daman shi yasan za’a rini tabbas yasan za’ai haka wanan yarinyari yasan daman tana da matsala daga an mata maganar aure saita fita hayyacin ta tane mi ta kashe mutum,haka fa sukayi da alhaji d’an gaske shima Allah ne ya cece sa mtwssss...

Tashi yayi ya d’aukko mota a daidai nan momy ta sakko tana tambayar lafiya mai yake faruwa naga gidan gaba d’aya ya rikice ne?”
“Wallahi momy babu lafiya yanzu dai babu lokacin bayani NU’AIMA zan kai asibiti ku shigo mota INAYATULLAH mu tafi.”
Ita kuma NU’AIMAN mai yasameta,a sanina dai bata da iskokai bansani ba ko a makarantar kwanan nan ta kwasosu.”
“No ba aljanu bane,ta tsorata da abin da yake faruwa shine ta suma.”
Cikin hanzari kuwa tace inaytullah tahau mota su tafi insha Allah zata farfad’o bai kamata kuma su tafi su duka abar NI’IMATULLAH a wanan halin ba,tunda gaki ga paris nasan zaku kula da ita.”
“Tam shikkenan momy.” INAYATULLAH ta fad’a tana shirin yin kuka. Suna shiga ya figu motar kamar zai tashi sama saura k’iris ma ya buge mai gadin ya wuce....

Momy kuma ta koma b’angaren su NA’IM.. ta kamo NI’IMATULLAH inda take ta ihu can yacewa momy ta taimako masa da cup da ruwa,aikuwa nan da nan ta tashi ta daukko masa dan ta manta da wasu masu aiki ma a gidan saboda rud’ewa. Amsa yayi yafara karanto ayoyin Allah da kuma wasu addu’o’i da mussaman aka koya musu su lokacin da suna madina.




Wata katuwar muryace ta fara magana”kaiiiiii yaro kayi ganganci kayi mutuk’ar ganganci mai yasa zaka shiga harkar daba taka ba,kanaso ka rasa rayuwar kane kana karmin yaron ka dakai,to idan kana son kanka da arziki ka wuce kabarmu karka sake zuba mana wanan wutar idan kuwa kayi wasa. To tabbas zata tashi dakai.”
“Astagfutullah,kai wanene kuma sanan mai yasa a shigo jikin wanan baiwar Allah maine yasa ka shigo.”
“Nifa na dad’e anan kuma matata ce matatace dan haka ka fita daga sabgar mu Na fad’a maka.”

Tundaga haka NA’IM da ya sake tambayar sa bai sake magana ba haka kuwa yaci gaba da zuba mata ruwan addu’ar har ta kwanta tayi lakwab a cinyar momy.
“Momy tayi bacci ki kaita ta huta.”
Momy ce ta d’auketa takaita d’akin su NU’AIMA ta kwantar da ita akan gado sanan ta fito palor tace dashi”mai yake faruwa ne Na’im hankalina ya kasa kwanciya.”
“Momy wanan yarinyar fa a ina take kuma mai tazo yi nan gidan,sanan mai ya had’ata da paris har naga ta shak’e masa wuya,badan Allah ya aikoni yau nadawo da wuri ba da bansan mai za’ai ba.”
“Hmmm wanan d’in k’awar NU’AIMA ce wacce kullum take maka zancen ta.”
“NI’IMATULLAH right.”
“Eh haka ne,ashe baka manta sunan taba?”
“A’a momy yanzu dai ya fad’o min,gaskiya momy nayi mutuk’ar tausaya mata ganin halin data ke ciki yarinya k’arama amma ba wasu mugayen aljanune a jikin ta kina ji ma yana cewa matarsa ce tabbas momy fitar dasu ba abu bane mai sauk’i.”
“Amma NA’IM baka da taimakon da zaka mata,wanan d’in k’awar y’atace tamkar y’a nake d’aukarta ganin yadda suna shak’u da NU’AIMA dan haka kaima ina so ka d’auke ta tamkar k’anwa kaji a ranka kamar NU’AIMA ce take cikin wanan halin zaka nema mata taimako.”
“Insha Allah mom saidai akwai wani abokina da mukayi karatu tare zan masa magana amma ba’anan garin yake ba yana maiduguri amma insha Allah ko zuwa bayan bikin su NU’AIMA ne komai zai warware Allah ya bata lafiya.”
“Ameen ya Allah Ameen,yawwa kira min INAYATULLAH naji yanda sukayi a asibiti.”
D’aukko wayar sa yayi ya danna kiran INAYATULLAH tana d’agawa da kuka a bakinta tace”hello bro ka fad’awa momy cewa ciwon NU’AIMA mane ya tashi yanzu nan kikita yake sanar samu dan hakaa sun shgar da ita I.C.U.”
Innahlillahi wa’inna ilahi raju’un gamu nan zuwa.”
Momyce ta kalle sa tace mai kuma ya faru?”
“Ciwon NU’AIMA ne ya tashi.”
“Innahlillahi wa innailahi raju’un,ya ubangiji kana ji kana gani ubangiji ka dafawa wanan yarinya ubangiji ka bata lafiya ka shiga lamarinta,bara na daukko mayafi na mu tafi sanan ka kira daddyn ku kafad’a masa.”
Sama ta haura ta d’aukko mayafin tana sakkowa a daidai lokacin NI’IMATULLAH ta juya,ta sake juyawa sai ta farka ganin ba d’akin ta bane kuma ita kad’ai a d’akin gaskiya ma bata gane ko ina bane si kawi ta fito tana dube dube,taga momy ta sauka zata fita a palorn da sauri ta sakko tana sakkowa tana tunawa da abin da ya faru tace”momyyy.”
“Juyowa momy tayi sanan tace”kin tashi NI’IMATULLAH.”
“Eh nastashi ina NU’AIMA?”
“I’m sorry NU’AIMA tana asibiti ciwon ta ya tashi.”
“Ciwo kuma,wane kalar ciwo ne da ita.”
“Shigo mota babu lokaci,”
Bayan sun shiga motar ne ta kalli momy hankali a tashe tace”momy dan Allah wane kalar ciwo ne da NU’AIMA?”
“NU’AIMA tana fama da ciwon zuciya tun batafi 12 years ba hakan ya bayyana,haka dai muke treating d’in ta bama son abin da zai d’aga mata hankali ko damuwarta.”
“Innahlillahi wa innailahi raju’un,ciwon zuciya kumwa wayyo Allah na,aikuwa sai ta fashe da kuka sosai momy ce take ta lallashin ta,shidai yana tuk’i hankalin sa gaba d’aya Yana asibiti wajen yar uwarsa...

 Basu jima ba saigasu a asibitin saboda babu nisa da gidan nasu,shiga ciki sukayi inda suka nufi b’angaren da ake kaita take ganin likita,likitan suka gani ya kwatanta musu inda suke... suna zuwa sukaga su paris da INAYATULLAH sunyi carko carko a tsaye ita kuma tana ciki an saka mata oxygen tana kwance a gado ba’a bawa kowa damar shiga ba..
Momy ta zauna akan kujera tayi tagumi tana karantawa NU’AIMA addu’o’in samun sauk’i,itama NI’IMATULLAH zama tayi akan kujera cikin mutuwar jiki da rasa yak’ini tama rasa yanda zatayi da rayuwar ta...

Suna nan haka rai babu dad’i har ishsha’i tayi inda suka nufi massalacin dake asibitin dan yin sallah jikin paris gaba d’aya ya mutu yama rasa mai yakamata yayi yasani idan NU’AIMA ta mutu to babu makawo shine ya kasheta ya rasa yaya zaiyi ma da rayuwar sa ne... shida NA’IM sukayi sallah inda  daddy ya k’araso kenan dan kuwa baya garin. Tambayar NA’IM ya fara yi kamar haka”NA’iM wane irin ganganci ne da rashin tunani irin naku ya jawo haaka?”
Gaban paris sai bugawa yake kamar ana buga ganga saboda tsoro kar NA’IM yace wani abu..
NA’IM yace”wallahi daddy bansan abin da ya faru ba nadawo daga wajen aiki ne kawai ina shigowa gida ita kuma zata fito a daidai k’ofar palor ta zame ta fad’i,daga nan ta suma ma,da muka kawo ta kuma likita ya gwada ta yace ciwon tane y tashi.”

“Sakacin banza da wofi,wallahi kasani NA’IM, PARIS DA MOMY naji wani abu tsab’anin haka wallahi sai ranku ya b’aci Na fad’a muku.”


Yana fad’ar haka ya koma reception ya zauna....
