Wani ɗaya daga cikin yayunta maza wanda shi ma Malam mai salatin ne ya haifeshi asali ma Baba Delun ce ta haifeshi, cikin tsananin fushi ya fisgota daga ita har ƴarta ya jawota ƙiiii har waje ya wurgar da ita, ALLAH ya taimaketa ta yi saurin ɗora hannunta ƙasa amman duk da haka sai da kan ƴar ya bugu da kanta lokacin da ta kai kasa, ba shiri yarinyar ta fashe da ƙara cikin sauri Aisha ta miƙe, tana kallon yayan nata ido cikin ido hawayenta suna kwaranya tamkar famfo wasu bayan wasu, yayan nata ya nunata da hannu cikin tsananin fushi yace

"WALLAHI kika kuskura mahaifinmu ya mutu ta sanadinki sai na kasheki"

A'isha ta yi masa wani kallon da shi kanshi sai da ya ji a jikinshi, sannan ta share hawayenta tace,. "ga ALLAH na dogara kuma gareshi nake neman mafita a duk lokacin da na shiga cikin ƙunci irin wannan da ma wanda zai fishi a gaba idan ya shigo rayuwata, saboda nasan cewa shi ALLAH baya zalunci kuma ya yi hani da aikatashi tsakaninmu bayinshi, saboda haka da sannu ALLAH zai yaye mani duhun da ya lulluɓe rayuwata, nasan irin wannan ranar zata zo ranar da gaskiya zata yi halinta, don gaskiya ɗaya ce kuma ina da tabbacin zata fito ranar da tafin hannu baya iya kareta, na yi imani da mummunar ƙaddarata kuma na ɗaura ɗamara zan saka hannuna biyu in rungumeta, amman abu biyu ne nake jin har zucina bazan iya aikatawa ba, saɓon ALLAH da gangan da kuma rabuwa da mahaifina da duk runtsi bana jin zan iya yin nesa da shi, na fi son kaf duniya mutanenta su tsaneni su zageni matuƙar shi yana saka mani albarka, haka zalika kuma har abada bakina bazai daina tsinewa duk wani mai hannu cikin wargaza mani rayuwa ba....."

Wani kuka ne ya sarƙeta wanda dole yasa ta dakatar da maganganunta, waɗanda duk kalma ɗaya da ke fita a bakin nata dai-dai take da saukar ruwan dalma a jikin Sanusi, cikin borin kunya ya buga mata wani uban tsaki yace

"Idan kin cika kangararriya to don ALLAH ki dawo gidan, ni kuma ki ga abunda zan yi maki wawuyar banza baƙar munafuka gantalalliya"

A'isha ta share hawayenta tace, "bana tsoron duk wani abu da zai sameni yanzu, don duk tozarcin da ake yiwa ƴa mara gata a gidanmu anyi man shi, abu ɗaya ne zai hanani shiga gidan a yanzu saboda in ragewa Babana zafin raɗaɗin takaicin da na ƙunsa masa, amman ka rubuta ka ajiye WALLAHI ni A'isha daga ni har ƴata bazamu yi nesa da gidanmu ba balle asalinmu"

Tana ƙare maganar ta nufi gidansu ƙawarta Maryam da gida ɗaya ne ya rabasu, yara ko suka biyota a baya ɗiii ana yi mata waƙa 

"A'isha ta yi cikin shege ta haifu Babanta ya korota"

A'isha ko gabanta bata gani jefa ƙafafuwanta kawai tana tafiyar  saboda tsabar tashin hankalin da take ciki, sai dai tun a soron gidansu ƙawarta Maryam Iya Talatu mahaifiyar maryam ɗin ta sha gabanta tace

"Ina zaki je kuma? ke da Mahaifinki ya kora a gidanshi to mu kuma me zamu yi maki a yanzu?? Haka kawai ki shafa muna kashin kaji, to bazaki shigar muna gida ba ki je cen wurin wanda ya yi maki cikin amman WALLAHI ba'a gidan nan ba, don ba zaki lalata muna sauran yaranmu ba, ke ma ɗin da kika lalacen ina yi maki addu'ar shiriya" 

A'isha saboda tsabar baƙin ciki kasa roƙarta ta yi ta juya ta nufi gidan wata ƙawar mahaifiyarta lokacin da take raye, sai dai tun kafin ta je itama labari ya je mata tana ganinta ta ce

"A'isha ALLAH ya isarwa Binta gareki kinci amanar naƙudar haifuwarki da ta yi a banza, ba ki saka mata da komi ba sai da guzurin baƙin ciki da abun kunya"

A'isha wani marayan kuka ya suɓuce mata ta kwance yarinyar da ke ta mutsunniyar yin kuka,  ta sami gefen ta 
zauna, ta ciro nono ta ɗora mata a baki tare da basmalah kamar yanda taga ake yi wa sababbin jarirai, ba shiri yarinya ta yi caraf ta karɓe tana tsotso cikin sauri, A'isha ta runtse idonta tana jin tsotsar tamkar ana fisgo ranta, yaran da suka biyota da ma ita kanta ƙawar mahaifiyarta sai kallonta suke yi ko ƙiftawa babu.

Ba shiri wani tausayi ya mamaye zuciyar Mama Ramatu, wani kuka ya suɓuce mata saboda tsabar tausayin A'ishar da jaririyarta, ta kori yaran ta nufi madafi ta ɗora ruwan zafi sannan ta dawo ta zura hannu ta karɓi yarinyar, tana ƙare mata kallo tana girgiza kai ta saka gefen zane tana share hawayen tausayin Aishar  da suke ta sauko mata a fuska, ta goye yarinyar ta nufi madafin tana ƙara ganganɗa wutar saboda son da take yi ta yi sauri ta ruru ruwan su yi zafi.

Yaran unguwar sai  leƙowa suke yi suna kallon A'ishar, inda wasu ƴan bani na iya kuma har cikin gidan suke shigowa suna ɗaukar A'ishar hoto a wayoyinsu, a ƙoƙarinsu na son su tura ta a media su bada labarin haifuwar ƴar shegiyar da ta yi.

Har sai da Mama Ramatu ta hankalta tace da A'ishar ta shiga ɗaki sannan ta fara balbale mutanen da faɗa tana korarsu, amman duk da hakan itama sai da suka ci nasarar ɗaukarta hoton tare da jinjirar da ke goye a bayanta, da ƙyar ta korasu da icce tamkar wata sabuwar mahaukaciya sannan ta rufe gidan ta dawo. 

Ta koma madafi ta taradda ruwan sun yi zafi ta cika ƙatuwar roba ta kaiwa A'isha ruwan bayinsu, ta katso iccen dalbejiyar da ke tsakar gidan nasu ta haɗa kansu waje ɗaya ta ƙulle sannan ta umurceta da ta je ta yi suraci, bayan ta gama ne ta ce da ita ta yi wankan jego kamar yanda kowace mai haifuwa take yi, A'isha cike da jin nauyi take wankan Mama Ramatu da kanta ta nuna mata yanda ake yi sannan ta fito bayin,  ta dawo madafin saboda ruwan da ta sake zubawa a tukunyar sun yi zafi ta zuba a bokiti ta ja bahon wankan jarirai da ke ajiye gefen ɗakinta ta yi cikin ɗakin da shi, sannan ta ɗauko haɗin sabulun da take yiwa ƴarta ƴar shekara biyu wanka, ta sauko da jaririyar ta fara yi mata wanka kamar yanda ake yi a al'adance, sannan ta saka sabulun ta darje yarinyar tass ta ɗebo gawayi ta fara gasa mata cibiya.

A'isha ta shigo ɗakin ta sami gefe ta rakuɓe Mama Ramatu tace, tashi daga nan ga kayan shafa cen ki ɗauko ki shafa, A'ishar ta miƙe zuciyarta a cunkushe ta fara shafa mai wanda shi kaɗai ne ma ta ji a ranta zata iya shafawa, jaririyarta kuwa saboda jin daɗin gashin jikin da ake yi mata ta langaɓe tana ta baccinta.

Bayan ta ƙare ne ta shafeta da mai har tana walƙiya da maiƙo, sannan ta ɗauko tsohuwar ajiyar kayan sanyin ƴarta na jarirai ta sakawa ƴar Aisha ta yi shif-shif da ita masha ALLAH ta sha kayan sanyi abinta har da su safa, sannan ta sake goyata ta ɗaukowa A'isha tufafin budurwar ƴarta Amina ta bata tace ita ta saka kafin Maigidanta ya dawo asan abun yi.

A'isha ta karɓa ta saka jefi jefi tana share hawayenta da har lokacin basu daina zubowa ba, Mama Ramatu tana kwashe kayan da ta yi wa yarinyar wankan ne ta ji bugun gida, ta yi saurin zubar da ruwan a makwarari ta saka hijabinta ta je ta buɗe ƙofar gidan, ashe yaranta ne suka dawo islamiyya amman har lokacin mutane ciccike a ƙofar gidan nasu,, aiko suna ganin an buɗe gidan mutane da yawa suka nufo ƙofar inda wasu ma su ke ta ƙoƙarin tureta su shiga ta ƙarfi, kasancewar itama jarumar mace ce yasa yaranta suna shigewa ta sake dantse gidan da sakata, daga inda take tana jiyo sautin wasu tsageran samari da  suna faɗin

"Dole ne fa sai mun shigo don mu ganewa idanuwanmu muma, muna nan ba zamu je ko'ina ba har sai an fito da ita munji dalilin haifa muna ƴar shegiya cikin unguwa"

Ta ja yan ƙananan yaranta zasu koma ciki budurwar ƴarta ta biyota  bayanta tana faɗin

"Wai Mama da gaske A'ishar gidan Baba Maisalati ta haifu kuma cikin shege??"

Mama Ramatu ta yi saurin juyowa ta ɗage hijabin da ya lulluɓe kariyar tace "kinga ƴar nan ma a bayana, amman kada in sake jin irin wannan maganar ta fito a bakinki, saboda kowane bawa yana tafe ne da ƙaddararsa, to ita tata ƙaddarar ce ta zo da hakan, ALLAH Ubangiji ya ƙara karemu da ku da kowa ma gaba ɗaya, ita kuma ya yi mata mafita a cikin lamarin nata"

Budurwar mai suna Amina cikin sanyin jiki tace,.  "Ameen Mama WALLAHI na tausayawa A'isha saboda baki ji yanda ake ta yamaɗiɗi da zancen ba, wasu ma har cewa suke yi ai wannan ba shine na farko ba wai asirinta ne ya tonu yanzu"

Mama Ramatu ta ji ranta ya ƙara ɓaci cikin fushi tace,. "ba nace kada in sake jin wannan maganar a bakinki ba Amina?"

Aminar ta yi saurin dafe bakinta tace, "Na tuba Mama ni ma ji nayi an faɗa"

Mama Ramatu ta maka mata harara tace,  "To kada inji kin sake faɗa matuƙar ba alkhairi ne zaki faɗa bakinki ba, saboda ke kanki shaida ce akan kamun kai da addini irin na A'isha, wanda kaf kafatanin unguwar nan babu wata yarinya da zata kamo ƙafarta akan natsuwa in dai har za'a tsaya a fayyace gaskiya, don haka ina roƙon ALLAH da ya saka mata ga duk wanda ya yi mata wannan zaluncin"

A'isha da ke ɗaki tana jiyo firarsu duk da a hankali suke yin maganar, ta yi saurin fashewa da wani sabon kukan tace,.  "kaico duniya rashin gata bai yi ba a rayuwa, don ni ma shi ne ya janyo man shiga wannan gararin duniya, ALLAH ka yaye man duhun da ya lulluɓe mani idanuwa ka yi man gata ko da na rasa kowa nawa a doron ƙasa"
