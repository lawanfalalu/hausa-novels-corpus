  *Page 11-12*

Yana shiga ya iske su zaune su uku,Hajiya,Safiya,Maryam suna ganinsa suka yi shuru da alamar akwai wani abu da suke qumsawa saboda yana shugowa dukansu suka yi shuru suka zuba masa na mujiya.
    Guri ya samu tare da zaunawa yace"Hajiya a gaskiya bana so Asma'u ta ci gaba da aikin wahala domin tana d'auke da juna biyu,idan kuma har Asma'u zatayi to dole sai-dai su dunga yi tare da Safiya domin ba finta komai tayi ba..." Hajiya ta katseshi ta hanyar d'aga masa hannu.
     Tace"Wallahi Abdulhamid in har ka k'ara maganar Asma'u bazatayi abinda nace ba,to sai ranka ya 6aci sai nayi mummunar sa6a maka shashanshan banza wanda mace ta gama dashi,abinda ta qumsu maka kenan ko？mijin tace？" Hawaye ne ke zuba fuskar Abdulhamid ya rasa ma abin cewa a hankali ya miqe ya d'an rankwafa yace"Hajiya a huta lafiya ni zan fita" kaida fuska tayi tare da tsuke baki,ba yarda ya iya ya juya ya fita.
.....................
Tun daga wannan rana Asma'u ta koma yin aikin gidan kamar can baya,sai-dai tana samun temakon Hajara wacce ke zuwa a 6oye tana temaka mata idan ta dawo Makaranta.
     Hajiya sun yanke hanyar da zasu zubar da cikin Asma'u,sai-dai har yanzun abin yaci tura yaqi gaba,sun zuba mata a abinci sun sa mata a ruwa haka hanyar da zata bi ta taka amma ba nasara abin kamar a shuka dussa,ba wani cigaba.
       Domin a yanzu haka cikin na Asma'u ya fito sosai har yafi na Safiya girma ita mai wata takwas na Asma'u wata Shidda. Ganin aikin da sukewa Asma'u yaqi yi sai suka yanke shawarar yima Abdulhamid suka je gurin wani boka masha hurin boka na kan tsauni suka amso magani domin su saka qiyayyar Asma'u a zuciyar Abdulhamid.
     Ai kuwa aiki sunyi nasara domin Asma'u ta fi Abdul kula da addini da addu'o'i wani lokacin shi yana da sakaci sai Asma'un ce ma ke nusar dashi.
   Lokaci guda Abdulhamid ya juyawa Asma'u baya ko maganarta bayayi baya kulata baya zuwa part d'inta komai nasa ya koma akan Safiya wacce yanzu yakewa wani tattali na musamman yayin da yayi watsi da Asma'u da duk wasu al-amuranta. Hajara ce kawai ke kwantar mata da hankalin don sai tayi sati bata gansa ba,bata sanin fitarsa bata sanin dawowarsa domin yanzu ya tare can a gurin Safiya,wacce duniya tawa dadi.
  Ana cikin haka Safiya ta haihu ta sami santalelan d'anta namiji ba laifi kyakkyawa da shi yayo tsawo na Abdul yayin da fuskarsa ta Safiya ce. Murna gurinta da Hajiya da Abdulhamid da Maryam ba'a magana sosai ake hidima yaron ya sami gata matuqa.
   Ranar suna kuwa anyi suna na kece raini inda aka gayyato makid'a da mawaqa akasha shagali yaro ya amsa sunansa NABIL,Hajiya kuwa atamfofi da les-les ta dunga rabawa jama'ar da sukazo suna da sauran kayayyaki irinsu sweet da minti da bairo mai sunan Nabeel sai qatuwar jaka mai d'auke da photon NABEEL ABDULHAMID MANI gaskiya abin yayi kyau da burgewa. Haka taro ya tashi lafiya kowa ya koma gidansa cike da farin ciki, gata sosai mutanan gidan ke nunawa Nabeel kowa qaunarsa yake.
   Bayan haihuwar Nabeel da wata biyu ita ma Asma'u ta fara naquda ciwo sosai ta ke hakan yasa aka kaita asibiti,bata san wanda ke kanta ba zubar jini kawai take da qar likitoci suka tsayar da jinin sannan suka ce dole a mata aiki ba yarda suka iya haka Abdulhamid yasa hannu duk da baya cikin hayyacinsa sosai Asma'u ke basa tausayi don taci wahala bata san wanda ke kanta ba Hajara idan banda kuka da addu'a babu abinda take don ko asibitin ita ce ta kawota sai daga baya ta kira Abdulhamid d'in.
    Hajiya Saratu ce ta shugo asibitin nan ta sami gu tayi parking sannan ta fito ta nufi cikin asibitin,Hajara ce kawai zaune kan benci ta gaisheta tace"ina Hajiya ta ke？.
    Hajara tace"tana cikin office d'in likita" tace"to zo muje ki rakani" babu musu ta taso tabi bayanta.
  Sunzo shiga kenan suka tsaya sakamakon maganar da sukaji Hajiya Mariya nayi da likita qasa-qasa ji sukayi Hajiyar na ce"Likita kasan dai yarda mukayi da kai bana so kowa yasan da wannan maganar zan biyaka ko nawa ne bana qaunar naga yarinyar nan Asma'u ta haihu tare da d'ana na tsaneta！natsanets！！bana qaunar naga sun rayu da d'ana har su haihu tare！Hajara ta dafe qirji hawaye na zubar mata jin abinda likitan yace yasa qirjinta tsananta bugawa domin cewa yayi...
    Likitan yace......"

Likitan yace"kar ki damu Hajiya domin mun gama komai mun riga mun tsara komai tuni kuma mun aikata kamar yarda kika buk'ata. Hajiya cikin jin dad'i tace"yawwa Likita haka nake so kuma naji dad'i komai ya tafi kan tsari da k'warewa sannan kasan dai wannan sirri ne tsakanin ni da kai bana son kowa ya sani"  (Kallonta nayi ni Fatima nace"Hajiya har da Fan's d'ina？wani kallo da ta jefomin ya sani juyawa ban shirya ba domin bairona sai da ya fad'i saboda tsoro kunsan dai halin Hajiya Mariya. Lolx)
    Likita yace"kar fa kiji komai irin wannan aikin ba akanki na fara ba kuma ina samun nasara." tace"yawwa nawa ne kud'in aikinka？d'an murmushi yayi yace"Hajjaju kenan kin mance yarda mukayi da ke ne？ai dubu dari biyar ne" tace"shi kenan babu matsala kazo gida ka sameni." 
   Yace"godiya nake Hajiyarmu."
   Hajara wacce ta sank'are a tsaye domin su sunji abinda mu bamuji ba,da gudu ta juya ta nufi d'akin da aka kwantar da Asma'u,kai tsaye gurin da aka shinfid'a babyn ta nufa,domin an mata aiko tuntuni,kallon babyn tayi tare da zaro ido,tabbas ba wannan babyn bane aka ciro a cikin Asma'u ba domin ita kad'ai taga babyn kuma zata iya gane sa don ko mahaifiyarsa bataga yaron ba kasancewar bata cikin hayyacinta har yanzun ma bata farfad'o ba.
    A furgice ta sake fita daga d'akin kai tsaye office d'in likitan ta nufa,Hajiya Saratu na biye da ita domin tare ma suka koma dakin da Asma'un take ciki.
     Babu Hajiya ta wuce kamar wark'iya ba wanda ya ganta,suna shiga Hajara ta chakumi wuyan likitan domin sam bata cikin hayyacinta,magana take tamkar wata ta6a66iya.
     "Ina babynmu ya ke？wallahi tallahi na rantse da Allah idan wani abu ya sami yaronmu kaima sai ya same ka domin wallahi babu mai rabani da kai sai hukuma."
   Hajiya Saratu tace"sakar sa Hajara ba ta nan zamu 6ullo masa ba ai maganin kar ayi to kar asoma ta inda aka hau tanan ake sauka kuma daman tsuntsun da yaja ruwa shi ruwa ke doka" hannu tasa cikon jakarta ta ciro bandir uku na dubu d'ari-d'ari.
     Tace"Likita ga wannan zan k'ara nunka maka kamarsu idan har ka dawo da yaron nan sannan karka damu zaka iya amsar na hannun Hajiya Mariya kuma bazata san komai ba." Kasancewar Doctor Kabir mutum ne mai son kud'i da son zuciya ga kwad'ayin tsiya ai kuwa hannunsa na rawa ya amsa yace.
    "Ku biyoni"
  Can wani d'aki suka je ya amshi yaron hannun wata nurse ya basu,Hajara na ganinsa ta tabbatar da shine hannu na rawa ta amsa ta k'amk'ame shi tana kuka sosai kamar ranta zai fita. Yaron Asma'u santalele kyakkyawan yaro sak ubansa Abdulhamid kamar yayi kaki ya tofar. Hajiya Saratu tacewa likita "Mun gode" ta basa katinta tace yazo ya sameta domin amsar cikon,ba su koma asibitin ba kai tsaye gidan Hajiya Saratu suka wuce kar ku mance ita ce mahaifiyar Safiya.
   Hajiya Saratu ta koka sosai akan rashin imani na Hajiya Mariya.
Hajara kuwa tana damk'e da yaron jin sonsa a cikin ranta ta ke ainun.
    Kamar yarda Hajiya Saratu da Hajara suka shawara akan ba wanda zaiji wannan labari domin sun san idan Hajiyar taji to bazata bar raye ba saboda a rashin imaninta zata iya kashe yaron hakan yasa,ba wanda zai sani hatta kuwa uwar yaron Asma'u sun bari zuwa wani lokaci idan k'ura ta lafa.
     'Yar aikin Hajiya Saratu me suna Uwani ta kira ta bata yaron tare da madararsa domin wani asibiti suka je bayan sun fito inda sukawa likitan k'aryar mahaifiyar yaron ce ta babu kuma mai shayarwa wacce za'a ba don haka ya d'aurasu akan madarar da ta dace nan take kuwa ya rubuta musu tare da k'a'idojin da za'abi don samun lafiyar yaron mai inganci,haka suka nunnunawa Uwani sannan suka koma asibitin.
   Suna shiga suka iske d'akin cike,Hajiya da Maryam da Safiya ga Abdulhamid da 'yar aikinsu Lantana,Asma'u kuwa tana zaune ne babu abinda take sai kuka jikinta har rawa ya ke yi.
   Hajiya Saratu ta k'araso tana cewa"me ke faruwa ne？Hajiya Mariya ta ya mutsa fuska tare da jan tsaki mtsuww tace"wai kawai saboda yaron da ta haifa ya koma take wannan kukan sai kace kanta farau？"
Hajara ta nuna kamar bata san komai ba da sauri ta k'arasa gurin Asma'u ta rungumeta ita ma tana kukan tana cewa"kiyi hakuri haka Allah ya nufa,Allah ya baki wani cikin gaggawa" wani kallo Hajiya ta zuba mata tare da cewa"to ba amin ba munafuka,ke baki tausaya mata irin wannan wahar da tasha ai kuwa dai ta huta ko？,Hajara bata ce mata komai ba.
   Kwanan Asma'u biyar aka sallameta,ta k'ara komawa shuru-shuru,ta sami lafiya jikinta yayi kyau sosai sai-dai nonuwanta sun cika sosai sai zubar ruwa suka hakan yasa suka bata magani,sannan suka wuce gida.
.....................
A gidan Hajiya Saratu akabar yaron Asma'u,wanda Hajara ta sakawa suna Nasir,Naseer sai wayansa yake yayi 6ul6ul tubarkalla madarar da ake basa ta amshesa sosai,shekararsa d'aya idan ka gansa sai kad'auka yayi biyu,gudunsa ya ke ko ina yaro kyakkyawa mai natsuwa don tun yana yaronsa bai da kazar-kazar irin na yara natsattsene ga tarbiyyar da ya ke samu.
   Shekararsa biyu a duniya Maryam da Hajara aka musu aure,kasancewar dukansu a cikin garin na KADUNA suka yi aure yasa Hajara ta nemi Hajiya Saratu akan ta bata yaron,ai kuwa haka akayi Naseer ya dawo hannun Hajara.
   Alhaji Sadeeq mijin Hajara yana matuqar qaunar Naseer ya d'auki Naseer tamkar d'an da ya haifa.

A 6angaren Asma'u kuwa. Wahala kala-kala ta ke sha,Abdulhamid ya dena kulata harkokin gabansa kawai ya ke sai kuma ta Safiya da yaronta Nabeel,Zuwa lokacin Nabeel shekararsa biyar a dai-dai lokacin ne kuma Safiyar ta masa k'anwa ta mace wacce ta ci sunan Hajiya wato Mariya sai ake kiranta Ilham,dai-dai lokacin ne kuma Maryam ma ta haifi 'yarta mace ta amsa Yasmeen.
    Hakan yasa Hajiya da su Safiya suka qara d'aurawa Asma'u karan tsana wai ta zama juya tunda bata sake haihuwa ba,ko 6ari bata sake yi ba,duk kuwa da sun san cewa Abdulhamid baya shiga part d'inta yanzu bare ya kwana tsantsar son rai da zalunci kawai.
   Hajiya ta tada fitina wai sai Abdulhamid ya saki Asma'u,shi kuma yace ina bai san da zan can ba,hakan yasa su komawa gurin bokansu inda ya basu wani magani akan idan har suka shafawa Asma'u a fuska to da Abdulhamid ya kalleta zai iya sakinta a take,haka suka dawo suna murna,Hajiya ta saka 'yan aikin gidan suka riqe Asma'u ta ba Safiya ta shafawa Asma'u duka fuskarta tana kuka da komai,su kansu 'yan aikin saboda basu da yarda zasuyi ne yasa suka riqe Asma'un. Bayan ta gama suka tasata gaba zuwa parlour domin lokacin Abdulhamid yana gida,d'agowar da zaiyi kuwa sukayi ido hudu da sauri ya sauke kansa qasa qirjinsa na bugawa da qarfi,Hajiya tace.
    "Abdulhamid idan ka cika d'an cikina kuma d'an halak mai bin umarnin mahaifiyarsa to ina so yanzu ka saki Asma'u！"
   A razane ya d'ago ya kalleta idanuwansa sun taru da ruwa ya sake kallon Asma'u wacce ke kuka kamar ranta zai fita,cikin kukan tace"Abban Nabeel ka bi umarnin mahaifiyarka ko don ka sami rabo mai yawa a gaba,na yarda na amince ka sakeni idan shine farin cikin mahaifiyarka" hawaye kawai akaga na bin kuncin Abdulhamid ya kasa magana bayan bero da takarda da ya d'auko ya rubuta _"Ni Abdulhamid Mani na saki matata Asma'u Haruna saki d'aya"_ kasa bata yayi ya ajiye takardar a gurin yana kuka kamar ransa zai fita ya tashi ya fice daga parlourn kamar walqiya.
   Kuka sosai Asma'u tayi ganin yarda aka shiga tsakaninta da Abdulhamid,haka ta hada kayanta tana kuka sosai su Hajiya da Safiya da Maryam da tazo suka rakata har bakin gate suna zaginta Safiya ce mai cewa "yau dai mun rabu da qaya sai aje can a qarata sai abi wani sarkin kuma."
   Hajiya tace"uban waye kuma zai iya kwasarta to qilan talaka d'an uwanta ya kwashi ragowa"
   Maryam tace"ayi gaba dai mun rabu da ala qaqai"
  Cikin kuka take kallonsu sai turata suke Safiya ta an gazata ta fada saman wani dutse nan take gefen goshinta ya fashe ta saki wata qara mai firgitarwa tare da fashewa da kuka,Hajiya tasa mai gadi ya rufe gate wanda saboda tausayi sai da yayi hawaye,haka ma'aikatan gidan sai kuka suke ko fitowa sun kasa saboda karma Hajiyar taga kukansu wani bala'in ne kuma.
  Da kyar Asma'u ta tashi tare da jan akwatinta tana kuka da komai ta tari napep ya kaita har qofar gidansu sannan ta sauka ta biyasa,cikin gida ta nufa sai ta iske wani tashin hankalin domin Malam ta iske babu lafiya abu duk yabi ya had'e mata. Watanta hudu da komawa gida Allah yayiwa Malam cikawa tayi kuka kamar ranta zai fita haka aka masa sallah maqota suka kaisa gidansa na gaskiya,gashi yanzu bata da kowa,ganin tana da wasu 'yan kudi gashi tana da sarqoqin gold da Abdulhamid ke saya mata sai ta sayar da daya da kudin ta koma makaranta daman tayi SSCE a daddafe don haka ta shiga University domin takardunta sunyi kyau sosai duk da ta gwamnati tayi amma Asma'u irin yaran nan ce masu kwazo.
........................
Naseer yanzu shekararsa bakwai ya girma sosai yaro kyakkyawa son kowa qin wanda bai samu ba. A dai-dai lokacin ne kuma Hajara ta haifi santaleliyar d'iyarta mace Naseer kuwa sai murna ya ke ya sami qanwa,sai-dai tun da akayi haihuwar Hajara ta saka Alhaji Sadeeq mijinta d'aukar Naseer ya kaiwa Hajiyarsa shi,kasancewar Alhaji Sadeeq yasan komai gami da Naseer  ita ma Hajiyarsa tasan komai kuma tana matuqar qaunar Naseer d'in hakan yasa ya kaisa gurinta tayi murna sosai domin daman ba wani yaro gabanta.
    Ranar suna yarinya ta amsa sunan Asma'u domin Hajara ce tasa Alhajin ya samata wannan sunan sai ake kiranta da Saanah,yarinya kyakkyawa sak ubanta da yake su d'in buzaye ne sai harta fi uban kyau tamkar Hajiyarsa ga gashi kamar me haskenta irin na larabawa sai-dai muce masha'Allah.
   Tofa tunda Naseer ya koma gidan Hajiyar Alhaji Sadeeq sai tace ita fa ta riqesa sai-dai su hakura su bar mata,sosai hakan yayiwa Hajara dadi domin daman a tsorace take zaune da shi ba don komai ba sai don 'yan uwanta musamman Maryam Safiya da Hajiyar dake zuwa gidanta har shi kansa Abdulhamid wani lokacin idan ya biyo hanyar yakan shugo su gaisa to ko da yaushe tana cikin nuku-nukun boyesa don kar su gansa sai taji dadi tayiwa Hajiyar godiya sosai.
  Alhaji Sadeeq ya saka Lameer wata tsadajjiyar makaranta Primary sannan ya saka shi islamiyya,sannan ya d'aukar masa direban da zai dunga jigilar kaisa da d'auko sa.
  Sosai Hajiya Karimatu ke kula da Naseer fagen gyarasa da tattalinsa ga ingantacciyar tarbiyya,da haka har lokaci yazo ya kammala primary sannan ya shiga qaramar secondry wato Jss1.
    Bangaren su Hajiya Mariya kuwa......

*Page 17-18*

A 'bangaran su Hajiya Mariya kuwa,suna nan lafiya,Nabeel da Ilham duk suna zuwa wata tsadaddajiyar makaranta,Nabeel kuwa gatan da ake nuna masa yayi yawa. Kakarsa tana matu'kar 'kaunarsa sam yanzu ta manta da wata Asma'u bare kuma d'an da ta haifa.
   Shima Alhaji Abdulhamid wanda shekarar da ta wuce suka je shida Safiya Maryam da kuma Hajara suka sauke farali,Sam ya manta da Asma'u duk da dai yana yawan mafarkinta ita da wani kyakkyawan yaro.
  Zaune suke dukansu a parlournsu da yasha gyara sosai da kayan alatu na zamani,Hajiya Safiya ce hakimce tasha wani ubansun leshi na alfarma,tayi wata muguwar qiba yanzu ta qara zama babbar mace,shima Alhaji Abdulhamid na zaune gefenta da jarida hannunsa,gefe guda Hajiya Mariya da ake zaune Nabeel kwance kan cinyarta,Ilham na zaune gefe daga qasa ita da Yasmeen da ta kawo musu ziyara,
  Hajiya Mariya tayi gyaran murya tace.
  "Abdulhamid Nabeel ya sameni ya sanar min England yake son zuwa domin cigaban karatunsa tunda ya ida secondary".
   Abdulhamid ya kalli Safiya yace" kinji abin da tace ya kika gani?"
  Hajiya Mariya tana matuqar jin baqinciki idan ta tambayi Abdulhamid Abu yanzu sai da izinin Safiya ya ke komai gashi bata da yarda zatayi yanzu domin komai da izinin Safiya ake gabatarwa yanzu.
