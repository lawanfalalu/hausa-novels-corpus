             SHAFI NA 'DAYA





_Bismillahir rahmanir raheem_




Rayam 'ya ce ga wani mutum wanda ake kiransa da Malam Balarabe, Malam Balarabe d'an garin kaduna ne, a nan aka haifeshi ya girma har yai aure da matarshi mai suna Saudat, Balarabe yana da yayyi da k'annai da wad'anda sukai aure da ma wad'anda basu yi ba, a cikin wad'anda basu yi auren ba mutum uku ne, biyu maza, da Bashiru da kuma rabe sai autarsu Hasiya.


Duk cikin 'yan'uwan Balarabe ya fi shak'uwa da k'aninsu Rabe, saboda ya fi yi mishi biyayya, kuma ya fi k'aunar matarshi da mutuntata, wannan dalilin ne yasa tasu ta zo d'aya, kuma ya fi sonshi fiye da sauran 'yan'uwan nashi.



Farkon zaman auren nasu sun farashi ne a gidan iyayenshi duk da kuwa kasancewar gidan cike yake da 'yan uwa, saboda a lokacin bai mallaki muhalli nashi na kanshi ba.



To kamar yanda muka sani ne zama tsakanin mata da miji ma kad'ai ya aka cika balle kuma cikin tsakiyar bataliyar 'yan'uwa ga matan yayyi da yaransu da iyaye.


Uwar miji tai mata nata, haka ma yayyinsa da k'anninsa maza, sannan duk yawan gidan nan ita take girkinsa gabad'aya, kuma ba yanda ta iya tunda umurnin mahaifiyarsa ne, amma da cin kashin ya cigaba da k'aruwa yana fin na baya yawa sanadin faruwar haka yasa Saudat ta matsawa mijinta akan lallai su tashi su sami muhalli, ba musu shima ya dage da nema domin yasan iya hak'uri matarshi nayi, domin wasu abubuwan ba sai an fad'a mishi ba yana gani da idanunshi.



A haka sukaita hak'urin zama a gidan da dad'i da ba dad'i har Allah ya azurtasu da 'ya mace, ranar suna aka sa mata Nana Khadija, bayan zagayowar wata shekarar aka sake haihuwar 'ya mace, ranar suna itama aka saka mata suna Nana Aysha, bayan haihuwar Aysha ne Allah ya azurtasu da gida nasu na kansu suka tashi suka koma can.


Zaria suka dawo da zama kasancewar aikinsa ya dawo nan, kuma baya so ya zauna kusa da 'yan uwan nashi sakamakon halayen da suke nunawa kan matarshi, kuma baya so sanadinta su rik'a samun sab'ani da su shiyasa kawai ya se gida a nan d'in domin yai nesa da su, amma duk da haka be tsira ba.


Bayan komawarsu suka samu wadataccen farin ciki, sai dai basu dad'e a cikinsa ba 'yan uwan Balarabe daga mazan har mata sai suka fara zuwa har gida suna ciwa matar mutunci  akan ta asirce musu d'an uwa, kuma muddin suna raye to ba zasu tab'a bari ta zauna a gidan nan ita kad'ai ba.


Da yake Saudat mace ce mai matuk'ar hak'uri da tawakkali akan komai, hakan yasa ko sau d'aya bata tab'a tanka musu ba, sai dai suyi su gama su tafi, ita kuma sai taje taita sallar nafila tana kai kukanta ga mahalicci akan ya karesu daga dukkan sharri.


To dama Allah ya fad'i muddin ka rok'eshi to ba zai tab'a baka kunya ba zai amsa maka, kasancewar haka ne yasa da sun fara yi mata gadar zare to da zarar ta rok'eshi sai ya kawo mata mafita cikin gaggawa sai abun ya koma kansu.



Haka rayuwa taita tafiya yau fari gobe bak'i, bayan shekaru Allah ya sake azurtasu da wasu yaran har hud'u, Maza uku sai  Rayam, bayan shekaru da haihuwarsu aka sake haifar yara mata biyu da Nana Fad'ima da Zainab, suka cika su takwas kenan daga nan ne kuma Allah bai sake ba Saudat haihuwa ba, k'ila Allah ya tsayar mata da haihuwar ne.


Haka Allah yake ikonsa, dole a cikin yaranka kaji kafi k'aunar wani fiye da saura, to haka ta faru a kansu, domin kuwa duk a cikin yaran da suka haifa sun fi k'aunar Rayam, tunda aka haifeta Allah ya dasa matsanancin k'aunarta a cikin zuciyar iyayenta, haka ma yayyinta biyar dukkansu suna matuk'ar k'aunarta.


Ranar suna taci suna Rayam, Allah yaiwa Rayam farin jini na ban mamaki, domin kuwa kowa so yake ya d'auketa, tana fara wayau kuwa cikin 'yan uwa suka fara rok'on a basu ita tayi koda kwana biyu ne.


A haka rayuwar Rayam ta fara cikin farin ciki, kowa yana k'aunarta saboda 'ya ce ita me shiga rai.


Kasancewar Balarabe mutum ne masani dai-dai gwargwado, hakan ne yasa ya san muhimmancin karatun addini ya saka yaranshi a islamiyya wadda da sun dawo boko zasu wuce islamiyya da an tashi islamiyya zasu wuce hadda, ba su zasu shigo gida ba sai magriba, hakan yasa rayuwarsu tai haske, kuma suka fita daban a cikin unguwa.


Nana Aisha da Nana khadija suna sauke Qur'ani da haddaceshi duba da yanda samari suka fara yi musu caaaaaa aka yasa Abbansu yace su fito da mazajen aure suna kammala secondry school zai aurar da su, in yaso sa cigaba da karatun a d'akin mazajensu idan mazajen sun yarje musu.



_Yana da kyau matuk'a iyaye su rik'a biyayya akan umurnin Allah (s.w.a), da kuma koyi da halayen manzo (s.a.w), ba don komai ba sai domin gujewa fad'awa ga azabar Allah ranar gobe kiyama, kai kanka sai an tambayi yanda ka tafiyar da rayuwarka, balle kuma yaran da Allah ya baka su ya damk'a amanar rayuwarsu a k'ark'ashinka, to dole duk yanda kayi da rayuwarsu sai Allah ya tambayeka saboda kasancewarsu a k'ark'ashinka, ya ka tafiyar da rayuwarsu, dan haka wallahi billahi sai mun tashi tsaye kan yaranmu, tarbiyyar yara babban jihadi ne, kuma ko ba komai idan d'anka ya zama shiryayyen bawa to ba kai kad'ai zaka yi alfahari da hakan ba har da mutanen unguwa da mutane daban-daban masu tarin yawa a wurare daban-daban saboda burgesu da yake da kyawun halinshi da kuma k'aruwa da suke ta sanadinshi, hakama idan ka bari ya zama lalatacce to ba kai kad'ai abun zai dama ba har da mutanen unguwa da kuma mutane da dama a wurare daban-daban, saboda munanan halayensa da kuma fitinarsu da zaiyi da mugun halinshi. Shiyasa ilimi yake haifar da cigaba a yayin da kuma rashinsa ke haifar da cutuwa, Allah yasa mu gane kuma mu gyara._



Hakan kuwa aka yi, bayan sun gabatar mishi da mazajen da suke son aura sai aka saka ranar aurensu, ya kama bayan gama secondry school d'in nasu da wata d'aya za'ai bikin.


Kwance tashi ba wuya a wurin Allah har ranar aure ta zagayo aka sha biki kowacce aka kaita d'akinta.


Bayan aurensu ba dad'ewa Allah yai wa mahaifiyar  babansu rasuwa wato kakarsu ta wurin uba.


Bata dad'e da rasuwa ba damuwar rashinta yasa shima mijin nata ya bita wato kakansu mahaifin babansu.


Bayan rasuwarsu ne Allah ya sake azurta Saudat da haihuwar mata biyu da Nana Fatima, da kuma Zainab, daga kansu ne kuma Allah ya hutar da ita bata sake haihuwa ba, a lokacin Rayam nada shekara biyar aka sakata makarantar boko da na islamiyya, wannan kenan.


Duk da kasancewar Balarabe ya bar family house d'insu be daina nuna kulawarsa ga 'yan'uwansa ba, musamman ma Rabe da tasu tafi zuwa d'aya, sai Hasiya, wasu suna farin ciki da kulawar wasu kuma rainawa suke.



Tun lokacin da zasu baro gidan su dawo nasu Balarabe yaso tafiya da Rabe amma sai ya nuna mishi baya so, shi ya fi son zama a nan, hakan yasa ya k'yaleshi.



Haka rabe ya taso cikin kyakkyawar kamala da mutunta kowa baida wani aibu a cikin halayensa, bayan ya  kammala karatunsa Balarabe ya samo mishi aiki ya fara yi duk wata ana biyansa albashi, sai dai har ya kai shekara arba'in da biyu be tab'a yin budurwa ba balle ma har a saka ran zai yi aure.



BAYAN SHEKARU ASHIRIN

Wata kyakkyawar yarinya na gani zaune tana duba litattafanta, kira naji an k'wala mata ana fad'in "Rayam kiyi sauri ki shirya fa lokaci ya kusa k'urewa."


'Dago kanta tayi tana kallon saitin k'ofar d'akin ta fara magana da muryarta me matuk'ar dad'in sauraro a kunnuwa, cikin natsuwa tace "to Ummi nah gani nan fitowa yanzu."


Baki da hanci na saki ina kallon kyakkyawar fuskarta mamallakiyar dogon hanci da manyan idanu kyawawa masu k'warjini, ga wushirya Allah ya bata had'e da dimple, baiwar sura kuwa Allah yai mata kyakkyawar sura da jiki me kyau, saboda duk da kasancewarta siririya to tanada nata baiwar jikin da Allah ya halitta mata su, Rayam ba fara bace bak'a ce, wadda bature ke yiwa irinsu lak'abi da black beauty, tana da murya me d'an karen dad'i wanda sauraronta kad'ai ya isa yasa ka sota balle kuma in ka zauna da ita ka fahimci hali da d'abi'u da kuma tsantsar kyawunta.


Cikin natsuwa take komai nata, shiyasa take burge kowa, tashi tayi tare da saka hijabinta har k'asa, sannan ta d'aura nik'ab d'inta, dama ta riga da ta gabatar da sallah, sai ta tattara litattafanta ta saka a jaka ta fito tsakar gida inda ta tarar da Umminta tana gyaran k'umbar k'afarta.


Duk'awa tayi kusa da ita tace "Ummi ba dai wani abinda zan miki kuma yanzu ko?" Ummi tai murmushi tana kallon yarinyar ta-ta, tace "kinyi komai Rayam, babu abinda ya rage, idan ma akwai ba zan barki kiyi ba saboda lokacin makaranta ne yanzu."


Rayam ta kalli rezar da ke hannun Ummi, sannan ta dawo da kallonta ga mahaifiyar ta-ta, tace "shine baki kirani nai miki gyaran k'umbar ba Ummi?"


Ummi tace "tashi ki wuce ki tafi makaranta 'yan'uwanki Fatima da Zainab sun dad'e da tafiya Rayam, kada kiyi latti sakamakon tsayawa wad'annan tuhume-tuhumen naki a kaina. Mik'ewa tayi tana murmushi, sannan tace "to Ummi ni na tafi sai na dawo," Ummi tace "Allah ya tsare, ya kaiki lafiya ya dawo dake lafiya, Allah ya bada abinda aka je nema Rayam, Allah yasa ku zama malamai."


Rayam tace "ameen Ummi nagode sosai da addu'o'inki," daga haka ta fice daga cikin gidan.


A k'ofar gida ta tarar da yayanta zaune shi da wani abokinsa suna hira, duk'awa tayi ta gaishesu cike da girmamawa, suka amsa sannan ta wuce.

             SHAFI NA BIYU




_Bismillahir rahmanir raheem_




Sai da Sudais yaga ta sha kwana sannan ya juyo da kallonshi kan abokin nashi wato yayanta, yace "gaskiya abokina muryar k'anwar nan taka akwai dad'i sosai wallahi, duk da ban tab'a ganin fuskarta ba amma kullum naji wannan zazzak'ar muryar ta-ta sai na koma gida da tunaninta, ka taimaka ka shigemin hanya na samu damar aurenta."


Ibrahim yai dariya, sannan yace "ba dai ni ba, zan dai baka damar ganinta amma wallahi ban maka alk'awarin yi mata magana a kanka ba."


Sudais yace "ai hakan ma nagode, insha Allah anjima zan dawo in ganta, yanzu bari na shige sai mun had'u anjima."


Nan suka yi sallama Sudais ya wuce gida shima ya tashi ya shiga cikin gida.


Da sallamarshi ya shiga cikin gida ya zauna kusa da mahaifiyarsu yana bata labarin yanda suka yi da Sudais, Ummi tai murmushi, sannan tace "to Allah yasa albarka ya tabbatar da alkhairi."


Ana gab da sallar magrib suka shigo cikin gida ita da 'yan uwanta, Ummi tai musu sannu da zuwa, suma sukai mata sannu da gida, hijabi kawai ta cire da nik'abi da safa ta fara alwala tare da umurtar k'anninta suma suje su yi.


Alwala suka yi ana kiran sallah suka yi sallah, bayan sun idar sunyi addu'o'i sai ta fara yiwa k'annenta muraji'ar karatunsu, bayan ta k'ara koya musu sannan tayi nata, a haka har aka kira sallar isha'i.


Ta idar kenan sai ga Ibrahim ya shigo cikin gidan d'auke da sallamarsa, suka amsa sannan suka yi mishi sannu da zuwa, kallon Rayam yayi yace "Rayam kije k'ofar gida zaku gaisa da abokina," Rayam da sauri ta d'ago idanunta ta kalli Ummi, itama Ummin ita take kallo, tace "tashi kije Rayam," ba musu ta tashi ta saka babban hijab d'inta, sannan ta d'auki nik'abinta ta b'oye, ta fito, tana zuwa zaure kafin ta fita ta saka nik'abin a fuskarta, tana sakawa sai ta fita a haka da shi a fuskarta.


Tana fita ta ganshi a k'ofar gidan nasu yana jiran fitowarta, bayan tai mishi sallama ya amsa sannan ta gaisheshi, kallonta ya hau yi, yaso ace yaga fuskarta kafin ya bayyana mata, domin tabbatarwa kansa yanda muryarta take shin haka ma fuskarta take da kyau sai dai ya makaro ta riga ta lullub'eta, murya ya gyara sannan ya cigaba da kallonta yace "malama Rayam wato ba komai bane ya kawoni wajenki illah so da k'auna, ina fatan zan samu karb'uwa a wajenki?"


Rayam kanta na k'asa tace "nagode da son da kake min Allah ya saka da alkhairi, dole akwai dalilin da yasa kake sona, meyasa kake sona, me ya jawo ra'ayi da hankalinka kaina?"


Sudais yace "ina ganin kamar baki shaidani ba, nine abokin Ibrahim yayanki wanda kike yawan gaishemu in muna tare dashi anan, kuma nine wanda kika gaisar d'azu, sannan maganar gaskiya duk da ban tab'a ganinki ba muryarki ta jawo ra'ayina kan sonki, abinda ma baki sani ba saboda in saurareta ne kullum nake zuwa nan k'ofar gidan."


Rayam tace "nagode da ka fad'amin gaskiya, amma kayi hak'uri hujjarka da dalilinka na sona basu gamsar dani a matsayin dalilan da nake son samu daga wajen mijin da zai zama abokin rayuwata ba, kayi hak'uri tunda ban mutu ba na tabbatar Allah bai gama min halitta ba, muryar tana iya dakushewa wata rana ko kuma ma in daina magana gabad'aya, ba yanda Allah bai canza lamuranshi, dan haka ina mai baka hak'uri tsakanina da Allah nake fad'a maka ba zan iya aurenka ba," Sudais yace "shikenan ba komai Allah ya zab'a mana abinda ya fi zama alkhairi, tabbas kin tunatar dani wani abu daga cikin iliminki, in kin shiga gida ki turomin Ibrahim d'in."


Nan suka yi sallama ta koma cikin gida ta sanar da Ibrahim sak'on Sudais, tashi yayi ya fita, suna zaune mahaifinsu ya shigo suka tashi suka yi mishi sannu da zuwa, amsawa yayi tare da samun waje ya zauna, Rayam ta kawowa mahaifinta ruwa, sannan Ummi ta bata izinin raba abinci, ta zubawa kowa ta rarraba sannan ta zauna suka fara ci, mahaifin Rayam yana cin abinci yana bawa Ummi labari, Rayam ta kalleshi tace "Abba ba dan na fika sani ba, a'a kawai zan tunatar da kai ne abinda ka sani tun kafin zuwana duniya, ba kyau muna cin abinci muna surutu, kuma duk abinda babu kyau in aka yishi hakan zai iya sa ka ka cutu, ni kuma banaso ka cutu shiyasa na sanar da kai kafin faruwar haka a gareka."


Abba yai murmushi, sannan yace "nagode 'yata, Allah yai miki albarka, cikin sauri ta d'ago ta kalleshi zatai magana sai yai sauri ya gyara zancen nashi yace af na manta ne Allah yai muku albarka gabad'ayanku," nan suka sa dariya dukkansu sannan suka cigaba da cin abinci ba tare da kowa ya k'ara cewa komai ba har sai da suka gama.


Bayan sun gama cin abinci Rayam da k'anninta suka kwashe abubuwan da suka ci abincin suka kai kicin, sannan suka dawo suka zauna aka d'an tab'a hira, a dai-dai lokacin yayanta Abdullahi ya shigo wanda daga matan nan biyu da aka fara haifa Nana Khadija da Nana Aisha sai shi, shima yayi aure gidanshi yana d'an nesa da na iyayenshi, kullum sai yazo ya gaishe da iyayenshi, in bai zo da safe ba ya kwashi gaisuwa kafin ya fita neman na abinci to zai zo da dare bayan ya dawo.


Zama yayi sannan ya gaisheda iyayenshi suma su Rayam suka gaisheshi, sun fara hira sai ga Ibrahim shima ya shigo ya gaishe da Abdullahi sannan aka zubo musu abinci tare da Abdullahin, ya ci kad'an sannan yai musu sallama ya tafi gida.


Rayam ma sai da safe tai wa iyayen nata sannan suka shiga d'akin da yake mallakinsu ita da k'annenta.


WASHE GARI
Idan Rayam ta tashi da asuba to bata komawa bacci sai ta gama duk ayyukan da suka dace da safe sannan in zata koma d'in ma zata kwanta, to yau ma bayan ta gama karatu gari ya fara haske ta fito ta fara aiki, bayan ta gama komai ta shiga wanka, bayan ta fito ta shirya, sannan ta tashi k'annenta suma suka shirya, fitowa suka yi suka tarar iyayensu sun tashi suka gaishesu sannan suka karya sukai musu sallama suka fita domin tafiya makaranta.


Sun zo bakin gate d'in makarantar zasu shiga Rayam ta cire nik'abinta kar a sake k'wace mata, suna shiga a dai-dai wasu had'ad'd'un motoci guda biyar a jere zasu fita daga makarantar.


Tsayar da motocin suka yi, Yarima ya gama k'arewa Rayam kallo har suka sha kwanar da zata sadasu da ajujuwansu, ya baiwa d'aya daga cikin dogaranshi umurni kan ya bita ya samo mishi bayanin inda take.


Ana tashi kasancewar suna tsayawa lesson shiyasa basa tashi da wuri, hakan yasa suka wuce islamiyya saboda lokacinta ya riga da yayi.


Bayan an tashi islamiyya sai suka wuce hadda, a hanyarsu ta zuwa hadda ne suka sake had'uwa da wannan  d'an sarkin da mutanenshi, sai dai wannan karon suna kan dokuna ne.


K'annenta kad'ai ya gani ya ganesu, gabanta suka sha, ba shiri su Rayam suka ja suka tsaya, sai wani daga cikin yaransa ya matso har gabansu ya kalleta yace "kije yarima yana kira," Rayam tace "makaranta zamu je, kuma bana tsayawa a hanya, yai hak'uri har mu tashi sai ya biyoni gidan iyayena."


Abinda tace ya dawo ya fad'a mishi, alamu yai musu da hannu akan su tafi, inda ya sa wasu suna bin su Rayam a baya har suka shiga cikin makarantar, su kuwa wad'anda suka biyo bayansu a dokunan suka tsaya a gefe suna jira a tashesu su bita suga gidansu kamar yanda  aka umurcesu.


Shi kuwa suna tafiya a hanya yana tunanin yarinyar, idanunshi ne kawai ke hasko mishi tsananin kyawunta, zai iya cewa tunda yake ma bai tab'a ganin kyakkyawar da ta kama k'afarta ba balle ta fita, sai dai baya son jan aji da rainin wayau, Yarima a ranshi yake ayyanawa banda yana k'aunarta da sai ya koya mata hankali.


Bayan an tashi daga hadda kai tsaye gida suka wuce.


Wad'annan mutanen suna ganin gidan da suka shiga sai suka juya suka tafi domin zuwa su sanar da Yarima cewa sun gano mishi gidansu.


Da isarsu suka sanar dashi, nan ya shaida musu cewa anjima kad'an zasu je gidan nasu ya ganta.


Bayan isha'i aka doka sallama a k'ofar gidan su Rayam, mahaifinta ne ya amsa ya fito, bayan sun gaisa sai suka shaida mishi abinda ke tafe da su, nan ya shaida musu cewa bari ya turo musu ita.


Yana shiga ya umurci Rayam da ta je k'ofar gida d'an sarkin garin nan yana son ganinta, amsawa tayi ba tare da musu ba, saboda tasan gab take da gama secondry school d'inta, dole itama ta fito da gwani tunda tasan haka tsarin mahaifinta yake kuma a kanta ba zata so a fara karya mishi tsarinshi ba, bayan haka ma itama kanta tana son taga tayi auren.


Hijabinta ta saka sannan ta  d'auki nik'abinta ta rik'e a hannu, tana zuwa zaure ta saka sannan ta fito.


Tana fitowa tai musu sallama suka amsa, sannan suka fara mata kirari gaisheki 'yar talakawa, wani kallo Yarima ya watsa musu, nan da nan suka canza taken kirarinsu suna fad'in "tuba muke yariman yarimomi, gaisheki gimbiyar yarima," gaisheshi tayi sannan ya kalleta yace "menene na saka wannan abun kuma a kan fuskarki bayan na riga da na ganki d'azu babu shi a makarantarku d'azu, maza cireshi kafin raina ya b'aci."


Ko motsi bata yi ba, k'ufula ya fara yi da halinta, hakan ya sashi yaye nik'abin da k'arfin tsiya nan da nan kyakkyawar fuskarta ta bayyana, murmushi ya fara saki, sannan yace "kyakkyawa dake amma kina b'oye kyawun fuskarki, koda yake hakan yayi min rana tunda da tuni na rasaki, ina son abu mai kyau kuma ke kyakkyawa ce, kamar yanda kika gani ni d'an sarauta ne d'an sarkin garin nan, gidanmu akwai alfarma, idan kika aureni zaki huta, bacci kanshi idan nai babu mai tashina, ga kuma bayi da suke mini hidima kema kina aurena zasu fara yi miki hidima, ina mai tabbatar miki da cewa in muka yi aure dake tabbas zaki ji dad'i."


Rayam tace "Kyawuna ya jawoka wajena, kuma wata rana zan iya wayar gari na rasa kyawun da ka aureni dominsa, kuma mulki ko daula basa gabana ni, kuma kace ba mai tashinka a bacci koda an kira sallah, dan haka in na aureka zanyi nadama domin kuwa baka sallar jam'i, ina maka addu'a Allah ya baka wadda ta fini amma maganar gaskiya ni bazan iya aurenka ba kayi hak'uri, kuma ko babu duk wad'annan iko da mulki to akwai  wulak'anci da zan fuskanta a wajenka tunda gashi yanzu ka gwada a gabana tun kafin ka samu amincewar soyayyata."


Fadawa suka yo kanta kamar zasu daketa suna fad'in "hattara dai yarinya," hannu Yarima ya d'aga musu, suka ja baya suka tsaya, Yarima ya kalleta sama da k'asa sannan ya tab'e baki yace "kinci sa'a ina k'aunarki da sai naga waye ubanki a garin nan." mota aka bud'e mishi ya shige suka kama hanyar komawa gida.


Cikin gida itama ta koma tana jin takaicin yaye mata nik'abi da yasa hannu yayi, kuma wai a haka yake tunanin zata iya amincewa ta aureshi haka kawai tun yana umurtarta akan abinda suka dace wata rana ya tilastata ta aikata abinda ya sab'awa umurnin Allah, da wannan tunanin a ranta ta k'arasa ciki.

tabbas an umurci maza da su auri mace dan kyawunta, dukiyarta, da kuma nasabarta, da addininta, amma dan a nuna mana duk abu ukun suna iya shud'ewa wata rana a wayi gari babu su sai abu d'aya kad'ai shine addini shiyasa aka umurci da a auri ma'abota addini shi yafi, duk auren da zaka yi baka duba wannan to wallahi billahil azeem gaba sai ya samu matsala, kodai a zamantakewa ko kuma aita haihuwar yara marasa tarbiyya kamar iyayensu, zan yi aure amma sai mijin da ya dace da fad'ar addinina kad'ai zan aura, domin ba ana aure bane domin raya duniya da jin dad'inta kawai da sharholiya a'a, ana aure ne domin gina lahira da kyakkyawan gini mai kyau, duk wanda maganata ta b'atawa rai a cikinku ya yi hak'uri."

Sun dad'e suna tattauna abubuwa muhimmai wanda daga k'arshe suka yiwa juna sai da safe kowa ya tafi makwancinsa.


Yau Monday, kuma a makaranta an umurcesu da su taho da wuri saboda gwamna zai kewayo makarantar tasu, amma abinda zai kawoshi basu da masaniya akai sudai kawai an fad'a musu cewa su shigo makaranta da wuri domin a had'u da malamai da d'alibai a shirya yanda za'a tarbeshi.


Hakan kuwa aka yi, da wuri suka shirya suka tafi makaranta, suna saka k'afa ciki suka ji anata rangad'a kiran sunan Rayam Balarabe a speaker, cikin sauri suka k'arasa wajen asembly d'in, inda k'annenta suka shiga layi ita kuma ta haura sama inda ake kiranta.

Su kansu malaman hatta Principal mamakinta suke yanda ta sauya da larabci kuma babu takardar a hannunta, hakan sai ya burgesu matuk'a.


Dama yana tunkaro wajen kowa da kowa ya mik'e tsaye, yana zama Rayam tana cigaba da yi mishi barka da zuwa da larabci, idanunshi k'ur a kanta, shi gani yake kamar ba mutum ba, dan zai iya cewa bai tab'a cin karo da kyakkyawar yarinya irin wannan ba ga kyau ga zak'in murya, bayan ta gama ta sake d'orawa da turanci, a lokacin ne ya fara fahimtar me take fad'i.


Idanunshi suna kanta har ta gama ta zauna, nan ya shiga mata tafi sai gabad'aya aka d'auka, nan wasu d'aliban suma suka yi nasu, daga nan kuma Principal tai jawabin barka da zuwa sannan sauran malamai, sai shima ya fara nashi jawabin cikin harshen turanci, yana godiya akan irin tarbar da akai mishi, sannan ya d'auki alk'awarin fad'ad'a makarantar nan da gyaran dukkanin abinda ya dace, kuma bayan haka wannan yarinyar da ta tarbeshi ya bata kyautar million d'aya saboda ta matuk'ar birgeshi, sannan ya k'ara baiwa d'alibai shawarar su dage da karatu domin su zama wasu nan gaba, bayan ya gama aka hau mishi tafi."


Bayan an gama taron aka sallami yara kowa ya tafi aji, shima gwamna malamai suka rakashi ya shiga mota shi da tawagarshi bayan ya bada cek d'in kud'in da ya bawa Rayam suka d'auki hanya.


Suna mota har sun yi nisa da makarantar sosai sunma fara shiga daji tunanin yarinyar nan kawai yake, muryarta ke dawo mishi cikin kunnuwanshi, sannan idanunshi na hasko mishi kyakkyawar fuskarta, a tak'aice ma dai yarinyar gizo ta hau yi mishi.


Nan da nan ya bada umurnin a juya da kan mota su koma makarantar, nan suka juya motocin suka sake dawowa makarantar, sai dai yana dawowa Principal ke shaida mishi cewa k'anwarta Zainab bata da lafiya sun tafi kaita gida, nan ya buk'aci da ai mishi kwatancen gidan nasu, da k'yar suka samo adireshin gidan aka bashi suka kama hanya suka tafi, da kwatance har Allah ya kawosu k'ofar gidan.



Sallama suka yi a k'ofar gidan, gwamna yana hakimce a cikin mota inda jami'ai suka zagaye motar da yake ciki da kuma unguwar da bindigoginsu suna gadin rai d'aya.


Amsawa Ibrahim yayi, sannan ya fito, yana fitowa yaji gabanshi yai mugun fad'uwa saboda wad'anda ya gani a k'ofar gidansu da bindigu ga jiniyoyi na tashi, a hankali Gwamna ya zage gilashin motar ganin wanda ke cikin motar ya k'ara saka Ibrahim razana, ko da be tab'a ganinshi ido da ido ba amma ya sha ganinshi a kalanda da kuma TV, Gwamna ne ya yafitoshi da hannu alamar yazo, cikin k'arfin hali ya k'arasa, sannan ya gaisheshi ya amsa mishi, Gwamna yace "nan ne gidansu Rayam ko?"


Ibrahim yace "eh nan ne ranka ya dad'e, amma me ya faru, ko tai maka wani laifi ne?"


Gwamna yai murmushi, sannan yace "kwantar da hankalinka, da alkhairi nazo, babu abinda tai min, ina mahaifinku?" Ibrahim yace "baya nan ya fita wajen aiki," to kwatantamin wajen aikin nashi, sannan ka je ka kira min Rayam d'in." Bayanin wajen yai musu, Gwamna ya kalli wanda ke kusa dashi yace "ka rubuta?" Yace "nasan wajen Allah ya taimakeka ba sai na rubuta ba ma." Nan Gwamna ya juyo ya kalli Ibrahim tare da umurtarshi da ya je ya kira Rayam d'in.


Cikin sauri Ibrahim ya shiga gida a lokacin Rayam na d'akinta ta gama cire uniform kenan sai gashi ya shigo, mahaifiyarsu ya fara fad'a mawa, ita kuma ta kira Rayam d'in tana zuwa ta sanar da ita.


Rayam tace "ok bari naje Ummi," ba musu ta fita inda suka bita da ido har ta fice daga gidan gabad'aya.


Tana fita ta sake gaisheshi, ya amsa yana kallonta yana zuba mata uban murmushi.


Be fito daga motar ba, gilashin kawai ya zage ta nan suke magana.


Magana ya fara yi mata yace "nasan kin san ko ni wanene amma duk da haka zan sake miki bayani, ni d'an siyasa ne gwamnan garin nan mai ci yanzu, k'aunarki ce ta kawoni gareki, Rayam idan kika yarda dani to zaki sha mamaki, zan k'irk'iro albashi wanda duk wata zan rik'a baki, haka ma kwangila game da alawus duk zan baki su da dama."

Wani dattijo ta gani tsaye a jikin wasu dank'ara dank'aran motoci, gefe da gefensa kuwa masu tsaron lafiyarsa ne, a tak'aice dai kai da gani kasan babban attajiri ne ya ziyarci k'ofar gidan nasu.

Rayam tace "wato akwai bambanci ni da su kenan?" yace "k'warai da gaske, ai ke kalar jibgegen gida ne shima a G.R.A," Rayam tace "to a gareka sam ba adalci, a garesu su kuma kayi butulci kuma in da nice matsayinsu nima hakan zaka yi min, kuma ba zan jura ba, irinku ne ga daular an ajiye mata a gida ba'a basu su wadatu amma ana fitowa ana baiwa na waje, dan haka Baba kayi hak'uri ba zan iya aurenka ba."

Rayam tace "wani Alhaji ne wai yana sona," nan ta bata labari tare da d'orawa da fad'in "na rasa yanda akai suka sanni har suke sanin inda nake," Ummi tai murmushi, sannan tace "ba gashinan ya fad'a miki inda ya ganki ba, kuma Rayam gidanku ai ba wuyar samu zai musu ba, tunda dole kema kinsan bincike zasu yi, mu dai fatanmu kawai shine Allah ya baki miji na gari."

Rayam tace "to bawan Allah baka san kamannina ba, in ka aureni fa daga baya kaga ban maka ba fa?" Mujahid yace "banda abinki baiwar Allah wanda zai yi aure domin bautar Allah ina ruwanshi da duba kyau ko makamantansu? nafi son mai tsoron Allah, mai addini da biyayya, ni ko sunanki fa ban sani ba, abu d'aya ya jawoni gareki shine natsuwarki, kuma saboda kyautata miki zato yasa na amince da abinda na gani a tare dake na tako nazo inda kike, ni burina kawai na samu abokiyar rayuwar da zamu cigaba da bautar Allah tare."

Rayam tai murmushi, sannan tace "sunana Rayam," nan ta shige gidan ta barshi tsaye yana maimaita sunan yana jin dad'in furtawa, daga nan ya kama hanyar tafiya gida.

A razane Mujahid ya duk'a k'asa kusa da ita yana kiran sunan Allah yana kiran sunanta.


A dai-dai wannan lokacin Ibrahim ya fito daga cikin gida, nan da nan yai wajen da sauri yana fad'in "me ya sameta?" be tsaya sauraron amsar da Mujahid zai bashi ba ya sab'ata suka shiga da ita ciki.


Ganin yanda aka shigo da Rayam ya tayar da hankalin Ummi da kuma 'yan'uwan Rayam d'in, salati suka hau yi sannan Ummi ta hau tambayar menene ya faru da ita.


Kai tsaye Ibrahim d'akinta ya kaita ya kwantar sannan Mujahid da ya kutso musu gida ba shiri saboda rud'u ya hau labarta musu yanda abun ya faru.


Ummi ta sake d'ora wani salatin, k'annenta kuwa banda kuka ba abinda suke, shima Mujahid k'atonshi da shi kuka yake, Ibrahim ya d'auko wayarshi a aljihu ya fara lalubar lamba, Ummi tace "wa zaka kira Ibrahim?" Ibrahim yace "zan kira Abba ne Ummi," Ummi tace "a'a kar ka kirashi, hankalinshi ne zai tashi ka bari in ya dawo ya ji halin da ake ciki, yanzu kamata yayi ka kira Khalid ka fad'a mishi abinda ke faruwa ya taho gida yanzu."


Khalid Magungunan islamic suke siyarwa, sannan duk wasu alamu na sihiri ko sammu ko jinnu suna gane alamominsu kuma Allah yana taimakonsu ana dacewa, shiyasa Ummi tace a kirashi.


Cikin sauri Ibrahim ya kira Khalid, yana d'agawa ya fad'a mishi halin da ake ciki, yana gama sanar da shi sai yace mishi gashi nan zuwa.


Dariya ta fara da wata muryar da daga ji kasan ba ta-ta bace, sai tace "ko me zaku yi ku yi, amma ni ba zan bar jikinta ba, in kuma sulhu kuke nema to wannan auren a fasashi, in ba haka ba kuma to tabbas zamu kashe mijin."


Gaban Mujahid ne ya hau luguden fad'uwa, amma a zahiri sai yai murmushin yak'e, sannan yace "ai rayuwata ba a hannunku take ba, dan haka babu abinda kuka isa ku yimin wallahi sai abinda Allah ya hukunta zai sameni kad'ai."


Jikinta ta fara bugawa a jikin bangon d'akin, ko kafin su rik'eta ta jijji rauni, a yanzu kam hatta Ummi kuka take ganin raunukan da 'yarta taji, Ibrahim da Mujahid ne suka rik'eta amma duk da haka sai hantsilar da su take, musamman ma Mujahid da ta shak'e mishi wuya wanda da k'yar aka samu aka b'amb'areshi, k'anninta dake kuka suna ganin haka suka koma bayan Ummi suka b'oye saboda suma sun fara jin tsoro, musamman da suka ji muryar yayarsu ta sauya, sannan kuma gashi tana shak'a da wurgi da mutane.


Nan da nan Mujahid bayan ya d'an wartsake daga shak'ar da yasha sai ya fara kwararo karatu da k'ira'arsa me d'an karen dad'i, sai da suka sha matuk'ar wahala kafin su samu su saketa, tana dawowa hayyacinta kuma sai bacci ya d'auketa, ko kafin Khalid ya zo komai yayi dai-dai.

Ibrahim ya kalli Mujahid yace "sannu Mujahid, yace "yauwa," Ummi ma ta kalleshi tace "sannu da k'ok'ari, Allah yai maka albarka," Mujahid yace "ameen Ummi," kalanzir aka d'auko mishi Ibrahim ya sa mishi cikin ikon Allah aka samu jinin ya tsaya da zuba.

Khalid yace "hakane jarabta babu ta yanda bata zuwa ma bawa, sannan su jinnu ba sai shagalalle kad'ai suke kamawa ba, wani ma tun haihuwarshi suke tare da shi kuma ya tashi har ya girma sunanan dasu zai zama malami se dai dole a wasu abubuwa su rik'a tab'ashi."


Ummi tace "Innalillahi wa inna ilaihir raji'un!!!"


A haka Abba ya dawo ya samesu, k'arasawa d'akin yayi yana tambayar me ke faruwa.

Abba yai shiru zuwa wani lokaci, sannan ya kalli Mujahid yace "shikenan zan yi shawara da mahaifiyarta, kuma sai munga yanda jikin nata ya kasance tukun."


Mujahid yai wa Abba godiya sannan yai musu sallama ya fice daga gidan."

Lokacin biki sai k'ara matsowa yake, ita kuma Rayam sai k'ara ja baya da Mujahid take, domin ko son ganinshi ma bata yi, bama shi kad'ai ba duk maza tsoronsu take ji domin gani take 'yan iska ne, idan kuwa kaje kusa da ita to ranar ba zaman lafiya ko da kuwa Abbanta ne ko d'aya daga cikin 'yan uwanta maza, Umminta kawai take yarda taje wajenta.


Mujahid kuwa tun daga lokacin sai ya zamana duk ranar da yazo sai tayi ciwo a ranar duk kuwa da ba fita zatai ba amma duk da haka sai tayi ciwo in yazo k'ofar gidan, dalilin haka yasa aka dakatar da shi da zuwan, duk ya bi ya rame ya jeme yanda kasan shi yake ciwon.

Rayam tai rau-rau idanunta suka cika da k'walla, tace "ba komai," nan ya dameta da tambaya akan lallai sai ta fad'a mishi abinda ke faruwa, zuciyar Rayam ce ke sanar da ita cewa ta fad'a mishi kawai tunda kowa nata ya k'i saurarenta balle har a fahimci damuwarta, wata k'ila shi idan ta fad'a mishi Allah yasa ya samo musu mafita.


Da wannan tunanin ta kwashe komai ta fad'a mishi, yanayin Mujahid lokaci d'aya ya canza, ya girgiza matuk'a da jin wannan labarin, da ka kalleshi zaka san tsananin firgici da kishi ne lullub'e a zuciyarshi.


Magana ya fara da k'yar yanda kasan an shak'e mishi wuya yace "insha Allah wannan halin da kike ciki ya zo k'arshe Rayam, domin yanzu ina komawa zan samu iyayena a saka ranar bikinmu ko nan da sati ne ki tare a gidana, dan Allah ki k'ara hak'uri na d'an wani lokaci, kuma kada ki tab'a bari ya samu galaba a kanki, sannan shawarata a gareki shine tunda in kin fad'a musu basa yarda to ku kwana tare da d'aya daga cikinsu domin su tabbatar da gaskiyarki, ke ni ban ma yarda ki cigaba da kwana ke d'aya ba, shi kuma in me shiryuwa ne Allah ya shiryeshi, ya yaye mishi son zuciya ya kuma rabashi da sharrin shaid'an."


Rayam tace "hakane ka kawo shawara, insha Allah zan yi yanda kace d'in," nan suka cigaba da tattaunawa akan matsalar, Rayam tunda abun nan ya fara faruwa bata tab'a samun natsuwa ba irin na yau, saboda yau ne karo na farko da ta tattauna akan matsalar da wani kuma ya fahimceta harma ya fara bata shawara akai.


_Hattara iyaye sau tari yara zasu zo da magana mai muhimmanci  akan wani abu da ya damesu amma sai ayi biris da ita k'arshe sai wani ko wata a waje ne zai saurari matsalarta, to idan akai rashin sa'a ta fad'awa wad'anda basu dace ba sai su bata gurguwar shawara, Allah yasa mu gane._


Sun dad'e suna tattaunawa, sannan suka yi sallama inda ya k'ara jadadda mata akan cewa kada ta sake ta cigaba da kwana ita kad'ai ta amsa mishi, daga nan ta shiga gida shima ya wuce nasu gidan.


Washe gari ma haka ta kasance kwata-kwata bata zama a sasan in dai yana nan saboda tsoro.


Yau laraba, zaune suke a tsakar gida suna aiki suna hira, Rayam ta zuba uban tagumi tana tunani, domin yanzu a k'alla ya kai wajen sati biyu kenan yana yi mata irin haka, kusan ma abun nashi gaba-gaba yake, domin yanzu idan ya ga babu kowa a sasan sai ita kad'ai a d'akinta to k'ok'arin kutso mata cikin d'aki yake.


Ummi ce ta lura da ita tace "Rayam ya kamata ki rage damuwar nan da kika sawa kanki, kada ki je ki janyowa kanki wani ciwon."


Rayam ta kalli mahaifiyarta cikin damuwa tace "Ummi wallahi tallahi ba sharri nake yi wa Baba Rabe ba, kinsan baku tab'a kamani da laifin yi muku k'arya ba."


Khadija tace "waye zai yarda da maganarki to bayan kowa ya san halinki na tsoron maza, kuma duk kallonsu kike a 'yan iska."


Cikin kuka Rayam tace "dama haka abun yake a duk lokacin da wani ciwo daban ya kamaka sai a k'i baka kulawar gaggawa saboda ana tunanin ai jinnun ne dai, haka ma idan ka tab'a yin wani abu sanadinsu ko ka warke shima sai a k'i yarda da maganarka saboda tunanin ai sune ma yanzu kawai suke damunka, to haka kuke nunawa a kaina, ina fad'a muku abinda ke faruwa dani amma ku mara lafiya har yanzu kuke kallona, wasu sui min dariya, wasu su ji haushina, gabad'aya kun d'auki zancena a matsayin shirme." Tana kai nan a zancenta ta sake fashewa da kuka.


Anty Khadija tace "ba haka bane k'anwata, ba wai bamu yarda bane, mun yarda da maganarki, sai dai abin da kike tunani a kan Baba Rabe ba haka bane, shi so yake miki na 'ya da uba ya kamata ki fahimci haka."


Rayam ta goge hawayen da ke zubo mata, tace "shikenan, tunda baku yarda da ni ba to ina so yau Anty Khadija dan Allah ki tayani kwana a d'akin nan, idan sharri nake mishi zaki ji zaki gani, in ma akasin haka ne duk zaki gani, babu wata riba ko lada da zan samu idan nai mishi sharri."


Khadija tai murmushi, sannan tace "ban ce kina mishi sharri ba k'anwata, amma wallahi nasan ma ba haka bane, amma Allah ya kaimu anjiman, zan rik'a tayaki kwana ba wai dan in tabbatar ba a'a, sai dai saboda ki rik'a samun bacci, dan duk wanda ya kalli idanunki  kwana biyun nan to ya san sam bakya samun runtsawa, har 'yar fuska kikai saboda rama."


Mik'ewa tayi domin ta k'arasa ciki, baiwar Allah Rayam kafin ta shiga sasan sai da ta kalli Zainab tace "ina Raliya?" Zainab tai dariya, sannan tace "tana d'akinta," sai a lokacin Rayam ta nufi sasan zata shiga d'akinta, sai dai tana zuwa sukai kicib'us da shi a hanya zai fito ita kuma zata shiga, wayarta ce ta fad'i k'asa saboda tsananin razana.


Duk'awa yayi ya d'auko mata ya mik'a mata, sannan ya kalleta yace "yau karkiy bacci da wuri anjima da dare zanzo miki hira."


Cike da tsoro tace "to," sannan ya bata hanya ta wuce ciki tana shiga d'aki ta sakawa k'ofarta sakata.


Ashe Zainab k'arya take mata kenan Raliya bata gidan amma tace mata tana d'akinta, ta tabbatar ba zai sha gabanta haka ba ya tsare mata hanya muddin matarshi tana nan.


Wayarta ce tai k'ara, Mujahid ne ya kirata, tana d'agawa bayan yai mata sallama ta amsa yace "kina ina ne yanzu haka?" Rayam tace "ina cikin d'akina na kulle na saka sakata," Mujahid yace "ya kamata ki rage jin tsoronshi Rayam, kada yai amfani da wannan jin tsoron naki ya samu galabar cutar da ke, kuma in muna so mu samu galaba a kanshi dole sai kin rage jin tsoronshi, dan Allah ki kwantarmin da hankalinki kuma, ki sani cewa k'arya bata tab'a tabbata, kuma ki sani gaskiya da mabiyanta kad'ai ke nasara, gaskiyarka da jajircewarka da hak'urinka sune suke zamarwa bawa tsani a cikin rayuwa, kema na tabbatar sune tsanin nasararki da yardar Allah Rayam d'ita me gaskiya," Rayam tace "tabbas haka ne, da yardar Allah zan rage jin tsoron, banda abuna ma ni da nake tare da mahaliccina akoda yaushe, kuma ina ji a raina yana jin rok'ona kuma ina gani yana bani kariya ta hanyar amsa addu'a na," sun dad'e suna hira da shi, sai da yaji katinshi ya shek'e sannan suka yi sallama.


Tunda Rayam ta bashi labarin nan yaji sam hankalinshi ya kasa kwanciya, shiyasa koda yaushe cikin kiranta a waya yake yaji a wane hali take ciki, tausayinta yake ji matuk'a yanda yaga damuwa tasa duk ta rame.


Wajen mai gidanshi ya tafi a daren nan ya sanar dashi buk'atarshi na zuwa wajen iyayen Rayam akan maganar sa ranarsu, da kuma rok'onsu akan baya so a saka da nisa, nan yai mishi alk'awarin cewa da yardar Allah gobe zai je wajen mahaifinta su tattauna akan maganar.


Rayam kuwa tana d'aki cike da tsoro duk da ta jiyo maganarshi a cikin d'aki suna hira da Raliya, can sai taji ana k'wank'wasa d'akin, nan gabanta ya fara fad'i, jin muryar yayarta Khadija yasa ta samu natsuwa, tashi tayi taje ta bud'e mata k'ofar ta shigo, tana shigowa Rayam ta tura k'ofa tana shirin saka sakata sai tace "yanzu ya ji muryata ai, yana jin kin sa sakata yasan a nan zan kwana, ki bari nan da 'yan mintuna sai in sa sakatar da kaina."


Zama Khadija tayi tana duba wayarta, inda ita kuma Rayam ta jawo Qur'ani ta fara karatu.


Sai da Rayam taji bacci ya fara k'ok'arin farmakarta sannan ta dasa aya tai addu'o'i ta shafe jikinta sannan ta kwanta, tana kwanciya kuwa ta fara bacci hankalinta kwance saboda yau ta san ba ita kad'ai bace shiyasa sam babu fargaba a tare da ita.


Sai da Rayam tai bacci da dad'ewa sosai sannan Khadija itama ta fara jin baccin, ajiye wayar tayi tai addu'a itama ta kwanta.


Tana rufe idanu taji an zare sakatar d'akin Raliya an fito, ana fitowa aka tunkaro k'ofar d'akin Rayam a hankali, ji tayi an fara k'wank'wasawa, haba ba shiri ta bud'e idanu tare da dunk'ulewa waje guda yanda kasan an shigo cikin d'akin sai zare idanu take ita d'aya.


Ci gaba da bugun k'ofar akai, kallon inda Rayam take kwance tayi taga sai baccinta take hankali kwance, sai a lokacin ne kuma tausayin 'yar uwar ta-ta ya kamata, a zuciyarta tace dama ashe da gaske take, ashe irin wannan tsoron da fargabar take tsintar kanta a ciki amma sam suka kasa fahimtarta.


Can sai taji ya fara mak'e murya yana mata d'an kira, "Rayam ki bud'e k'ofar mana nine Babanki Rabe ba cutar da ke zanyi ba, kawai so nake na tayaki hira sannan mu ji dad'in daren ni da ke ta hanyar biyawa juna buk'ata," Khadija a zuciyarta fad'i take shikenan ta tabbata.


A tak'aice dai a ranar Khadija bata yi bacci ba, haka shima be yi bacci ba domin sintiri yaita yi ya kai ya kawo yana k'wank'wasawa, sai da asuba tayi sannan yai alwala ya fita masallaci a lokacin Khadija ta fito ta gudu d'aki sai dai tana tafiya ta fara tunanin to idan kuma ya dawo ya shiga ya cutar mata da 'yar uwa fa, hakan yasa tai sauri tai alwala sannan d'ebo wani ruwan  ta tahowa da Rayam itama ta nufo d'akin.


Tana zuwa a dai-dai Rayam ta tashi, dama ita sallar asuba da ma ko wace sallah bata wuceta, Khadija na shigowa ta dannawa k'ofa sakata, ruwan ta mik'awa Rayam tace "tunda ga bokiti can kiyi alwalan a d'aki kawai ba sai kin fita ba, bari nai sallah idan kin idar kema sai mu yi magana."


Rayam ta karb'i ruwa tai alwala tai sallah, ko kafin ta idar bacci ya sure Khadija saboda bata samu ta yi ba da daddare.


Itama Rayam komawa baccin tayi, wa yaga ba sabam ba yau Rayam har da samun komawa bacci.


Khadija ce ta fara tashi sannan ta tashi Rayam ta tasata suka bar d'akin sukai sasa domin gaishe da iyayensu.


'Dakin Ummi suka shiga, Abbansu ya riga da ya fita, Ummi suka gaisar ta amsa har tana musu wasan a kwana a hantse sai kyawawa suna murmushi.


Ummi ta kalli Khadija tace "ya na ganki yau kema kin zama wata salalau kin koma kamar 'yar'uwar taki Rayam?"


Khadija ta samu waje ta zauna, sannan ta kalli Ummi tace "hum ke dai bari Ummi, ashe Baba Rabe d'an iska ne? wallahi Ummi jiya duk naji abubuwan da Rayam ke fad'a mana bama yarda, Ummi har da maganganu masu girman fad'i ma a baki," nan ta ba mahaifiyarsu labarin abinda ya faru a daren.


Ta ci gaba da fad'in wallahi na tsorata sosai, sai gani nake kamar zai b'alla k'ofar ya shigo, ba ni nai bacci ba sai asuba, gaskiya Rayam ba zan k'ara tayaki kwana ba, kuma kema kin bar kwana ke kad'ai daga yau."


Ummi tai shiru tana nazarin maganar tasu, can sai ta d'ago kai tace "Allah ya kyauta," Khadija tace "ameen Ummi, amma abinda kawai zaki ce kenan Ummi? wannan ba fa maganar da za'ai shiru ba ce." Ummi tace "to me kike so in ce Khadija? 'yan uwan mahaifinku cewa suke bana son kowa ya zauna tare da ni, hatta zaman Rabe a gidan nan sai da suka yi surutun nan, na tabbatar in maganar nan ta fita cewa zasu yi naiwa d'an uwansu k'azafi, ke ba ma su ba hatta mahaifinku ba zai tab'a yarda ba wallahi yanda yai mugun amanna da halayen Rabe, ni kaina har yanzu ban gama yarda da maganganunku ba, amma idan halinshi ne zan cigaba da addu'a Allah ya bayyanashi a gaban kowa ya gani."


Ana cikin wannan maganar sai Baba Rabe ya k'walawa Fatima kira ta amsa, sannan ta kalli Mahaifiyarta tana zazzare idanu tace "ni wallahi tsoro nake ji ba zan iya zuwa ba," Zainab ta ja tsaki, sannan tace wallahi tsoron da ya ga ana ji nashi shi yasa ko ita Anty Rayam yake mata iskanci, wallahi ni bana jin tsoronshi, muje in raka ki, suka tafi ta rakata ashe aikenta zai yi shagon k'ofar gida, tare suka je suka dawo, kuma a tare suka kai mishi sak'on.


Allah sarki uwa, a zahiri ta danne damuwarta, saboda bayyanashi zai iya k'ara dagula zuciyoyin yaranta, amma a cikin zuciyarta Allah ne kad'ai masanin tarin adadin damuwar da take ciki akan wannan lamarin, gashi tashinsu daga gidan babu rana tunda yayi gidan kanshi amma dan tsabar mugunta ya k'i ya tashi ya bar gidan.


Da dare yayi Rayam ta nemi d'an tayin kwana ta rasa dan Khadija ta rantse ba zata k'ara tayata kwana ba a hanata bacci da buge-buge da tsoro. Zainab tace "muje Anty in tayaki kwana," suka yiwa Ummi sallama suka tafi, a tsakar gidan kuwa suka sameshi, ganinsu su biyu yasa be tanka mata ba suka shige suka kulle k'ofa.


Amma stil dai cikin dare sai da ya damesu da buge-bugen, wannan karon ma da k'arfi yake yi, inda suka tashi dukkansu suka k'ank'ame juna, Zainab me kurin bata jin tsoro ta fara runtuma kuka tana fad'in "ni wallahi ki tashi ki kaini wajen Ummi nah, wayyo Allah Ummi tsautsayi da k'arar kwana sun kawoni inda za'a kashe miki ni."


A wannan karon maimakon Rayam ta cigaba da damuwa sai ma dariya da Zainab ta bata, a hankali cikin rad'a tace "ina jarumtar taki ta tafi kuma Zainab?" Zainab tace "ana shirin kashemu ke kuma kina dariya a maimakon ki sakamu muita maimaita kalmar shahada ko."


Dariya Rayam take sosai, hayaniyarsu ce ta tayar da Raliya daga bacci wanda ya sata tasowa daga gado, jin motsinta ne yasa Rabe yin wuf ya shige d'akin.


Kallonshi tayi tace "lafiya nake jiyo hayaniyar su Rayam, kamar ma harda muryar Zainab ko?" Rabe ya ja tsaki, sannan yace "je ki koma bacci abunki, nima k'ararsu ce ta tayar da ni har na fita in ga ko menene ashe wai mummunan mafarki Zainab ta yi, shine take yin wannan kukan."


Raliya na jin haka sai ta fasa fita dama har yanzu da bacci a idonta, dan haka sai ta koma tai kwanciyarta.







_To gashinan dai a hankali halin Rabe ya fara fitowa fili mutum biyu sun ji zahiri, dama addu'a takobi ce ta mumini, gashi nan kuma muna ganin k'arfin tasirinta._

Daga wannan ranar sai yaran su kansu suka fara jin tsoron taya Rayam kwana saboda suna ganin zahirin yanda yake kai kawo, ga yawan kiranta da yake d'akinsu, gashi Raliya ta tashi ta je ta bashi lambar Rayam d'in kullum cikin damunta yake da waya ba dare ba rana.


Da abun ya kai mak'ura a wajen Rayam sai ta rage jin tsoronshi, kuma ko ya kirata bata zuwa inda yake tunda iskanci ne yake kawoshi inda take ko kuma in ya kirata babu magana ta alkhairi a ranshi sai sharri.


Yau ma ba wanda ya zo tayata kwana saboda yanzu tsoron sasan suke ji sosai, hakan yasa ta saka Fatima ta rakota ta shige d'aki ta saka sakata sannan ta juya itama da sauri ta koma nata d'akin.


Yau ma haka ta kasance, tun farkon dare ya fara buge-bugen k'ofa har tsakiyar dare, washe gari da safe kafin gari yai haske shi ya tasheta da buga k'ofarshi.


Ranta ne ya fara  b'aci sosai, ace da dare ba zai barta tai bacci lafiya ba da buge-bugenshi, sannan abun yanzu ya kai har da safe ma, wayarta ta d'auka ta danna lambar Umminta ta kirata, tana kira ta d'aga, tana d'agawa bata yi magana ba saboda idan tai magana zai ji kuma zai gudu shiyasa tai shiru, dan ita so take tazo sasan ta ganin wa idonta itama.


Ummi tana ganin kiran Rayam jikinta ya bata, sai ta taso ta fito ta nufo d'akin Rayam d'in, Baba Rabe yana jin an taho sasan yai wuf ya shige d'akinsu da gudu, Ummi ta k'araso k'ofar d'akin ta k'wank'wasa tare da kiran sunan Rayam d'in, Rayam ta amsa, sannan ta taso zata bud'e, tana bud'ewa taga bata nan har ta juya ta tafi, ganin haka yasa ta sake maida k'ofarta ta saka sakata.


Tana maida sakatar sai ya fito ya cigaba da bugawa yana kiran sunanta, sai Rayam ta sake kiran lambar Ummi, tana kiranta sai gata ta dawo sasan a dai-dai ta kamashi yana buga k'ofar.


Yana ganin Ummi yai sauri ya matsa gefe yana fad'in sannu Maman Rayam ina kwana," ta amsa mishi suka gaisa, suna gama gaisawa yai sauri ya shige d'aki. Ummi tana k'arasowa k'ofar d'akin ta kira sunan Rayam da k'arfi ta amsa kuma ta taso cikin sauri tazo ta bud'e k'ofar, sai a lokacin ne ta samu ta fito suka tafi d'akin Ummi a can ne ta wuni, ita da d'akinta amma sai dai idan baya nan take komawa.


Suna zuwa d'aki Ummi ta kalli Rayam tace "tabbas yau na ga zahirin abinda kika dad'w kina fad'a mana Rayam, wallahi banda na gani da idona ba zan yarda zai iya aikata haka ba, saboda kyawawan hali da yake nunawa a zahirinshi," Rayam tace "Ummi ki bar mutum kawai, amma mutane da dama a bad'ininsu munin halayensu da za'a ce ki kalla bakya iya gani saboda ya munana da yawa," Anty Khadija da ta shigo ta iske suna zancen tace "ni sai yanzu na gano dalilin da ya hanashi yin aure da wuri, Allah kad'ai ya san iya adadin mutanen da ya b'atawa rayuwa. Rayam ta kalli Khadija tace "Antynah kada ki d'aukarwa kanki kayan da zai miki wahalar saukewa dan Allah, manzo (s.a.w) yace zato zunubi ne koda ya kasance gaskiya, ki zama mai bada shaida a iya abinda kika gani kad'ai, ki daina yarda kina k'aras da iya abinda kike hassashe a cikin zuciyarki," sun dad'e suna tattauna wannan maganar, daga k'arshe suka fara haramar d'ora abun karyawa.


Tun daga ranar da idanun Ummi suka ganin mata zahiri yasa ta saka idanu sosai a sasan.


Da dare yayi Rayam na cikin d'akin ta tura k'ofarta amma bata saka sakata ba tana sallah ne niyyarta idan ta idar ta je taiwa Umminta da Abbanta sai da safe sannan ta zo ta kwanta, dama haka take yi kullum in har zata kwanta sai ta musu sallama, tana cikin sallar kawai sai ji ta yi ana k'wank'wasa mata k'ofa, Raben ne dai wai ta bud'e sak'o ya kawo mata, shi a tunaninshi da yaga ta turo ta saka sakata ne.


Cikin ikon Allah sai ga Ummi again ta k'ara dawowa a dai-dai lokacin ta sake kamashi, kawai sai ganinshi tayi a k'ofar d'akin 'yarta yana k'wank'wasawa.


Tana k'arasowa yai maza ya matsa kamar kullum yace "sannu maman Rayam," Ummi tace "yauwa, tana bud'ewa taga Rayam na sallah, sai tace tana sallah ne Rabe," Rabe yace "to ba damuwa."


Yana nan zaune a k'ofar d'akinsu itama Ummi ta samu waje a cikin d'akin ta zauna har ta idar, sannan bayan tai addu'a Ummi ta kalleta tace "tashi muje d'akina ki ci abincin a can."


Tashi suka yi suka fito daga d'akin suka wuceshi suka tafi d'akin Ummi.


Sai da Ummi ta kamashi wajen sau Hud'u yana bugawa 'yarta k'ofa, kuma hankalinta sam ya kasa kwanciya, tayi shiru ne dai kawai amma a zuciyarta tunanin mafita kawai take.


Yau dai Ummi suna zaune da Khadija a tsakar gida tana bata labarin kama Rabe da tayi har sau hud'u a k'ofar d'akin Rayam d'in.


Ummi ta cigaba da fad'in "yau zan kawo k'arshen wannan lamarin, dan gaskiya ko na kwanta yanzu bana samun bacci saboda tunanin a wane hali 'yata take ciki, ni kuma d'akina Abbanku baya son muna kwana da kowa a ciki tunda a d'akin shima yake kwana."
