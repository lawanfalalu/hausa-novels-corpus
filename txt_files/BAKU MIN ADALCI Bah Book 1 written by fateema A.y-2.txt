Babban gida neh ba laifi sai de bangare neh daban daban family house neh da ya hada yan uwa da dama.


4 Brothers house kenan

Akwai Alhaji Bala wanda shine babban su sai wanda yake binsa alhji Musa sai Alhaji Mus'ab. Alhaji Ibrahim shine qaramin su kuma shi neh  Wanda Allah ya karbi ransa da jimawa.


Ya bar yara guda biyu Khaleel da Kuhailat. 


Suna tare da mahaifiyar su cikin kwanciyar hankali da kulawa.


Duk da cewa suna fama da talauci kasancewar tunda mahaifinsu ya rasu babu wani xance na rabon gado tunda dukiyan hade yake.


Hajia Saudat mace ce me kwantaccen hankali sannan abun dunia be dameta ba tana daukan rayuwa da sauqi duk yadda taxo mata.


Kafin tayi wannan auren saeda tayi a wani gun, tana da yara guda biyar Allah yayi wa mijin rasuwa.

  Alhmdllh yaran nata duk sun girma kafin rasuwan uban nasu dan duk matan sunyi aure maxan ma dan suna karatu neh shiyasa basuyi ba har xuwa yanxu.

Shekarun su bakwai da aure Allah yayi wa shima wannan mijin rasuwa.


Sai de tunda yanxu girma yaxo shiyasa ta ci gaba da rainon yaran tah da basu kulawa duk da cewan tana ganin abu iri iri a gun kishiyoyin balbalin nata ammn hakan be sa tayi tunanin barin gidan bah.


Ba laifi akwai ci da sha kam ammn duk wata dawainiya basa mata shi hakan ne yasa bata cika xama bah take sana'a kuma yaran ta na taimaka mata.


Bata kuma taba magana akan a raba gado bah duk da cewa ta san mijinta yana da kudi. Amman tunda dukiyan a hade neh shiyasa bata taba yin magana da sunan a basu haqqin su bah.



Kuhailat na da 10 khaleel na da 13 years, dare daya mahaifiyar su ta farka cikin dare da matsanancin ciwo, nan fa ta fara kiran sunan yaran nata. Khaleel ne ya farka ta fada mar akan ya kira mata Hajia Maryam.

“Ka kirani cikin wannan tsohon daren? gaskiya baxan samu xuwa bah  saide in xuwa safia”.


Jiki a sanyaye Khaleel ya koma ya sheda wa mahaifiyar sah wannan, xuwa wannan lokacin ciwon ya qara tsanani.


Xama khaleel da kuhailat suka yi kusa da mahaifiyar su suna faman kuka tana son musu magana ammn ta kasa bbu jimawa rai yayi halinsa.****


Khaleel neh yayi hamdala shi a nashi tunanin bacci ne ya dau Umman su be san abun ba haka yake bah ***** 

Kuhailat ya kalla sannan   ya bata umarnin taje ta kwanta.


Bbu musu ta miqe ta koma bayan Umman tah ta kwanta.....


Wutan dakin ya kashe musu sannan shima ya nufi nashi dakin ya kwanta.


Ita ma Kuhailat bacci neh yayi awon gaba da ita sai dai tunda tayi baccin wuya kawae take sha dan munanan mafarkai take yi.


Kuhailat qaramar yarinya ceh ammn mai hankalin manya ko da ta farka addu'ah tayi ta tofa musu ita da Umman tah sannan ta miqe tayi alwala kasancewar asuba tayi.*****


Ba tayi yunqurin tashin Ummanta ba dan a cewarta xata barta ta huta bata da lpy.......

Khaleel neh yace Kuhailat ta fita taje a kai su schl shi xai xauna ya jira Umman tasu.***


Bbu musu ta fita, Amman koda ta fita ta samu driver ya kai yan gidan schl an barsu inda sabo sun saba wannan ba sabon abu bane agun su duk sanda basuyi saurin fitowa ba tafia akeyi a barsu  saide ummansu ta nemi kudin mota ta basu amman idan yan sauran dakin ne koda xa ayi latti baxa a tafi ba sai an jirasu.....


Komawa tayi ta shaidawa yayan nata sun tafi beyi mamaki ba tunda hakan ake musu dama****


Khaleel ne yaga shirun yayi yawa shiyasa yace Kuhailat ta jira shi bari yaje gun Alhaji babba ya sanar masa ko xa a kaita asibiti .....

Kukan yaran neh ya sata qarasawa cikin dakin da sauri a durqushe ta same su a gaban umman tasu sunata faman uban kuka****


“Subhanallahi Khaleel lafia meke faruwa kuke ta faman uban kuka? meyasa baku tafi makaranta bah?” ****


Shi dai khaleel be iya cewa komai bah.


Kuhailat neh tayi qarfin halin nuna mata umman su dake kwance bbu alaman motsi......


Da sauri Meroo takai dubanta gun, gaban gadon ta qarisa tana tambayan me ya same tah bbu wanda ya iya cewa komai......


Hannunta ta kai jikin Saude, a sanyaye ta cire hanun dan ta riga ta gane bbu rai sai de tana tsoron sanar da yaran dan ita kanta tana son yaran sabanin sauran na gidan da basu da kunya idan ta musu kuskure a gabanta suke tsayuwa su xageta tas saboda rashin tarbiya amman tana mugun jin dadin xama da yaran Hajia Saudat har wanda suke gidan auren su.....


Dakin ta bari da sauri ta nufi bangaren Hajia Zainab.....


Shiga tayi bbu ko sallama tana xare ido bakinta nata rawa....

Hajia Zainav ceh ta daka mata tsawa sai da ta raxana. “Ya dai lafia kika shigo min daki bbu ko sallama ko munafukan uwar dakin ki neh ta aiko ki kivgane mata meke faruwa anan din?”*****

“Hajia kiyi haquri  Saudat neh bbu lafia ina tunanin ma kaman ta rasu neh.....”


“Thou likita! keh har kinsan wani mutuwa mtsww gayyar tsiya ai duk abun daya neh saiki tashi muje.”


Ita dai Meroo tuni tabar dakin sabanin Hajia Zainab da saida ta gama gadaran ta sannan ta fito xuwa dakin Saude.


Ko da ta shiga sai faman toshe hanci takeyi wai dakin yana wari....

Meroo ta aika ta kira mata sauran matan gidan.

Da sauri tafia bbu jimawa ta dawo wai suna xuwa. Hajia Zainab bata wani damu ba. “Lallai matar nan bata da imani.” Meroo ta fada a xuciyrta.


Ba jimawa suma suka xo. Hajia Asiya kam ta kadu sosai da jin mutuwan Saude dan tasu taxo daya ba kaman sauran bah.

Juyin dunia anyi amman sam suka ce basu san xancen bah saida abun yayi yawa neh suka yarda qanin Saude ya dau namijin su kuma xasu bar macen agun su bbu yanda suka iya shiysa suka barsu kawai tunda su bawani qarfine dasu bah bare a ce in basu girmama su Dan Allah ba su girmama su saboda kudinsu ****


Koda suka basu namijin mah sai da suka musu gargadi akan in gado suke tunanin xa a bawa yaran yasa sukevson daukan su sun yi kuskure dan mahaifinsu be bar komai ba kafin mutuwan sa.....

Rayuwa kenan, yanxu mutane basu dau cin gadon marayu a bakin komai bah bare kuma ayi xancen sada xumunci ko kyautatawa abunda ka mutu ka bari. Zamanin nan kowa kansa da yaransa kawai ya sani bbu xancen bari na taimakawa dan dan' uwa nah tunda bashi da rai sai dai ma ina xan samu abunda ya barwa marayun na cinye***

Khaleel yaro neh sai de yana da hankalin manya.


Hakan kuma ba qaramin baqin ciki take bawa Zainab bah dan tafi so a kullum ya xama masifu na bin yarinyan shine xata samu kwanciyan hankali ga ilimin yarinyan yana bala'in damunta hakn yasa tayi shawara akan ta ringa hana yarinyan fita skul kullum randa xatan ma ya xama a latti ga kuma datti ammn duk a banxa har yanxu hakan be hanata qoqari a makaranta bah.
