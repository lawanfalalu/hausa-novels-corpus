Bayan sun sauke farali aka dawo Nigeria  da tsaraba ta musamman , lokacin Delu na fama da laulayi ko me taci babu daɗi ga yawan amai , gidan momy ta dawo ta tare har cikinta yakai wata tara ,cikin ikon Allah washe gari ta tashi da naƙuda ,ana shirin tafiya asibiti ta sunɓule yaronta bayan an kira norse ta tabbatar da lafiyar yaro da mai jego aka gyarasu tsaf ,kasancewar aihuwar  safe ce aka sa nata ruwan wanka mai zafi ta gasa jikinta aka yiwa baby wanda dama already Mk yayi siyayyar jirajirai har su momy suna ce nasa bashi da kunya .
Ranar suna yaro yaci sunan babab Delu wato Abdullahi , suna kiransa da Arif sosai suka dunga kula dashi ta ɓangarori ,A lokacin da Barira taga gidan da Delu take zaune sai da tayi kuka daɗi ,da ta tashi tafiya aka haɗa mata kayan abinci da kuɗaɗe Delu ma ta bata 50k ,da waya ƙarama ,kasancewar arziƙin su MK sai hau hawa yake komai suka jura ruba suke samu sosai.
