   Bayan sun nutsu guri guda sae Khausar taje kitchen inda ta d'auko musu abinci ta kai kan table,Sugrah tace "lallae y'ar tawa ta kusa kaiwa na aure har girki kke yi?",cikeda jin kunyar maganar Khausar tace "nikam aunty ban wani iya girki ba kwae naga Mommy bata nan neh shiyasa",Sugrah tace "toh Allah yasa yyi dad'i",ta fad'a neh cikeda zolaya,Khausar taje ta qarasa setting table d'in.Ana cikin haka saiga Rayyan ya dawo,Khaleel da Khazana neh suka fita a guje suna fad'in "oyoyo daddy!!oyoyo daddy",Khazana koh cewa tayi "ai ba daddynka bane daddy nane ni kad'ae",cikeda yarinta tayi maganar,Khaleel kuma abunka da shima ba wani wayon saeya ce "wallah qaryarki yarinya,daddy nane nima",fad'a suka cigaba yana fad'i tana fad'i har daddyn da aka jeh yima oyoyo ya shigo yyi parking harya fito daga motar ya nufo inda suke amma fad'a suke kamar ya'ya'n kishiyoyi 😂..



   "Haba Abbana wannan ba qanwarka bace kke sa'insa da ita",Khaleel yaje yayi hugging Rayyan da gudu yana fad'in "ae ban san ka iso bane Daddy,wae ce mun fa tayi kai ba daddy na bane",yah qarasa maganar cikeda hali irinta yarinta,Khazana ma da gudun tazo tana fad'in "ka sake mun daddy nah nikaaam",kamar zatayi kuka,Rayyan yah tsuguna a wajen ya janyo su dukansu jikinshi yana fad'in "kunga bana son rigima,daddynku neh ni duka kunji? So banda fad'a dan nazo da chocolates kuma bazan bama me fad'a ba",nan da nan Khazana ta sauko dan tana mugun son chocolate tace "wollaah daddy na bari,kuma ai nace na sanma shi kai d'in koh",wata irin dariya ta bama Rayyan wae ta sanmashi ni,toh ana sanmar da ubane?,a ranshi yyi maganar.Yace "toh shikenan mu shiga ciki sae munci abinci zan baku koh",bbu musu suka shiga yana riqe da hannun kowannensu abun sha'awa.



    Suna shiga kuwa saiga su Khairat ma sukayi mishi oyoyon kamar wani yyi tafiya meh nisa yah dawo.Sumayya da Sugrah sukayi mishi sannu da dawo wa,Sumayya ta karb'i jakar labtop dake hannunshi ta kai mishi d'akinshi,dawowa tayi suka had'u gaba d'ayansu sukayi having lunch,abun ya mugun bada colour.

     Da sallama ta qarasa inda yake jingine a jikin mota,yana ganinta shima ya fara matsowa kusa,ya ansa sallamar.Bayan sun gaisa saiya fara mata introducing kanshi kamar haka "da farko dai sunana *Suraij Muhammad*,mu y'an asalin Maiduguri neh amma yah kawo mahaifinmu nan,so anan muka taso nida wana mu biyu iyayenmu keda,na biyu kuma ina meh baki haquri kan bibiyarki da nayi tayi tun sati biyu kenan yanzu da suka wuce,bazan b'oye miki ba wallahi kinmun neh inasonki,dukda be kamata nazo gareki daga farko ba, kamata yyi na nemi izini amma kar yin hakan ya janyo wani abun shiyasa nace barin tuntub'e ki tukunna,dan Allah ki bani dama Sugrah",mamaki neh ya bayyana qarara a fuskar Sugrah jin ya kira sunanta,ya lura da hakan amma bece komai akai ba,"yah kkayi shiru malama Sugrah?".



   Sugrah dae ta rasa na fad'i daga bisani tace dashi "naji kuma na gode amma kafinnan inaso kabani lokaci nayi tunani akai tukunna,daga baya zan sanarda kai hukuncin dana yanke",tayi hakan neh saboda tasan kafin lokacin Sagir d'inta yah dawo kuma ya turo tunda yana dawowa za'ayi musu baiko,Surayj kuwa be damuba dajin hakan,kwae cewa yyi "toh Allah yah kaimu",tace "ameen".Bayan sunyi exchanging contact sae sukayi sallama ta shiga gida shi kuma ya shiga motarshi ya tafi cikeda jin dad'in yadda Sugrah ta karb'e shi,dan koh be sameta matsayin mata ba yasan ta mishi halacci.

   Ana cikin haka wayarta ta sake ringing a karo na biyu,koda ta duba sae taga numbern Sagir ceh,cikeda zumud'i ta d'aga dan a tunaninta zaece mata sun sauqo daga jirgi neh dan lokacin da zasu tashi ma ya fad'a mata,tana d'agawa tayi sallama,sae kuma taji sab'anin muryarda take sauraron ji,nan akayi mata wata mummunar sanarwa wanda hakan yasaka ta cikin tashin hankali a take.Zuciyarta bugu ta shigayi tana fad'in "no! Noh!!    Bayan mutumin ya sanarda ita saeya katse kiran,Sugrah tafi awa a wajen tana kukanda bbu hawaye dan hawayen ma gagararta yyi,daga qarshe tayi tunanin kiran Mamanshi dan sanarda ita daman tanada numbern ta sukan gaisa time to time hakannan.Tana kira taji mamanma cikin tashin hankali take dan taga sanarwar a tv,shi kad'ae takedashi daman sae qanwarshi wato Siyama,yanzu ita kad'ai takeda a duniyarnan,Maman tace da Sugrah sae haquri yanzu ma airport d'in zasu da Abbansu dan d'aukar gawarshi,daga qarshe tayi hanging call d'in.
