Hannu Malan Bukar yasa ya share zufar data zubo mashi,  kada dai ace Alhaji ya samu labarin auren dole zai yima Hamrah?  Gyaran murya Alhaji yayi yana fad'in Malan Bukar ko akwai matsala ne?  Murmushi Malan Bukar ya saki yana gyara zama yace bakomai Alhaji kawai ka tuna mani da Mahaifiyar Hamrah ne baiwar Allah mace mai kirki. 



Alhaji yace Hamrah itace d'iyarta kenan? Malan Bukar yace kwarai kuwa,  domin ita kad'ai ta haifa Allah ya mashi abunsa,  sai dai ko sau d'aya Hamrah bata tab'a kukan mutuwar Mahaifiyarta ba saboda Kubra ta had'ata da d'iyarta Hibbah ta rik'esu tamkar itace ta haifesu,  saboda son da take ma Hamrah ne yasa lokacin da Hibbah ta samu miji Kubra tace dole tajira itama Hamrah ta samu sai ahad'a tare, ganin shiru bata samu ba yasa na yanke shawarar baka aurenta domin nasan zaka riketa amana,  hakan da nayi sosai yayi ma Kubra dad'i,  shiyasa da taga an kawo kayan sa ranar Hibbah ba'a kawo na Hamrah ba ta shiga damuwa,  shine nace zanzo nayi maka magana sai gashi ma ka kirani.



Alhaji yace Allah sarki,  gaskiya samun irin su Kubra a wannan zamanin sai an tona,  Allah ya bata ladar rikon marainiyarsa da tayi. Malan Bukar yace amin. Alhaji yace dama akan wannan maganar ne yasa na kira ka,  insha Allah zuwa gobe zan aiko da kayan sa rana sai a bama Hamrah hakuri dan bazan samu zuwa ba idan aka kawota gidana sai muga juna. 



Malan Bukar yace haba Alhaji ai bakomai nima nafiso idan aka kawota kuga junan domin Hamrah yarinyar kirki ce,  kuma tana na'am da wannan auren sosai. Alhaji yace maganar kayan lefe kuma zan bada wasu kaya da zatayi amfani dasu wajen bikin sai a d'inka mata,  sauran lefen ba'a k'asarnan nakeso a had'o mata ba idan yaso bayan an kawota sai na bata lefen da hannuna. 



Saurin kallonshi Malan Bukar yayi jin abinda yace,  sam ba haka yaso ba,  domin Kubra tace idan aka kawo lefen Hamrah sai su rage wasu kayan su sayar tunda sunsan lefen Hibbah dole zaiyi tsada. 



Muryar Alhaji yaji yana fad'in ina fatan akwai telan da zai mata d'inki kamar kala goma a cikin sati hud'u? Murmushi Malan Bukar yayi yace sosai ma. Alhaji yace shikenan a goben za'a had'o da kayan insha Allah,  kuma ba mata bane zasu kawo zan aiko da babban yarona mai kular mani da shaguna shine zai kawo,  Bilal dan yana shirin tafiya waje da shi zan aiko amatsayinsa na babban 'Dah na namiji. Malan Bukar yace ai ba damuwa mungode sosai Allah ya k'ara girma. Dubu goma Alhaji ya bama Malan Bukar yana fad'in ga wannan kayi cefane. Godiya yayi masa ya tafi cike da farin ciki.

Sosai Umma taji dad'in kud'in da Alhaji ya bama Malan Bukar,  sai dai bataji dad'in rashin kawo lefen da zasuyi ba,  sosai taci buri akan lefen saboda tasan dole Alhaji ya saki kud'i yayima Hamrah kaya masu tsada,  amma bakomai ko bayan ya bata ne zataje ta d'auko abinda takeso idan ma hada zinari duk sai ta karb'o su. 


Malan Bukar yace yanzu Kubrah zanje nasiyo ko zabbi ne guda biyu a soya ma yaron Alhaji da zai zo kawo kaya kinga kada aka gazawar mu ko? Umma tace wannan haka yake,  dole sai munyi badda sahu akan wannan bikin na Hamrah saboda kada Alhaji da mutanan sa su fahimci wani abu,  bakomai zanyi komai amma dole na fanshe wahalata. 



Malan Bukar yace yauwa kinga bayan biki sai kije ki samo mana abinda ya bata,  domin yace lefen ma a waje za'a had'o mata,  gaskiya ba dan Hibbah ta samu miji ba da ita ya kamata ace ta auri Alhaji. 



Saurin tashi Umma tayi tana fad'in haba Malan,  taya zaka had'a Hibbah da wancan tsohon da yakusa yin jika da ita,  ai wallahi koda Muftahu ba mai kud'i bane Hibbah bazata auri tsoho kamar Alhaji ba,  ita dai wancan marar galihun banza taje tayi ta fama,  amma Hibbah sai sardidin saurayi matashi mai arziki Miftahu. 



Jiki a sanyaye Malan Bukar yake kallon Umma,  jiyayi wani kululun abu ya tsaya masa azuciya,  idanuwanshi ne suka ciko da ruwa,  nan take kanshi ya sara,  yana so yayima Umma masifa amma ya kasa domin asiri yana aiki akansa,  acan k'asar zuciyarsa yanaji bai kyauta ma Hamrah da Mahaifiyarta ba,  amma kuma baya iya magana. 



Ganin yayi shiru ne yasa jikinta yayi sanyi,  kada dai ace asirin jikinsa ya fara sanyi,  dan tunda ta zagi Hamrah taga yanayinsa ya canza,  k'arfin hali tayi ta sake d'aure fuska tana fad'in ashe Malan yanzu ka daina sona ko?  Shikenan nagode. Da sauri Malan Bukar ya tashi yana fad'in haba ni na isa,  kiyi hakuri idan na b'ata maki rai,  duk abinda kikace haka za'ayi. Murmushin jin dad'i ta saki jin maganinta har yanzu yana aiki,  sai dai dole taje a k'ara mata wani idan zata kai sunan Muftahu wajen boka domin yasa Hibbah ta mallakeshi. Kallonshi tayi tana fad'in shikenan ya wuce.

Hibbah tace Umma ki daina damuwa,  ni nasan waye Mufty,  tunda yace mani wajen da zamu zauna waje ne na musamman na daina damun kaina,  kuma yace sai ana saura kwana d'aya d'aurin aure za'aje ayi jere saboda baya son akai kayan b'arayi suje gidan tunda unguwar ta 'yan gayu ce. 



Umma tace haka ne kuma,  shikenan badamuwa,  duk wanda yayi mani maganar zuwa jere zan san abinda zance masa. Hibbah tace Umma kinsan halin 'yan uwanki da surutu haka zasuyi ta damunki da maganar lefe da kuma jere,  sannan 'yan unguwa ma damunki zasuyi tayi. 



Umma tace haka ne fa,  to ya kike ganin za'ayi? Shiru Hibbah tayi can kuma tace Umma kawai kije wajen boka kisa ya rufema kowa baki kinga babu wanda zaiyi maki magana. Murmushi Umma tayi tace yauwa 'yar gari,  shiyasa nake sonki domin baki baro komai nawa ba. 



Hibbah tace to Umma kije da wuri fa. Umma tace gobe kuwa zanje,  ba zan d'auki Laure ba kinsanta da surutu kada taje ta yad'ani agari. Hibbah tace yauwa. Umma tace tashi muje ciki yau babu abincin dare tunda akwai da yawa. Hibbah tace ni Umma Mufty nake son gani wallahi. Rik'e baki Umma tayi tana kallonta kafin tace ba d'azu kuka rabu ba?  Langwab'e kai Hibbah tayi tana fad'in haka kawai nake son na ganshi fa. Tab'e baki Umma tayi tace kinji dashi ni nayi ciki,  idan kikayi hak'uri ai ya kusa zama naki har abada. 


*****


Duk yanda Aunty Bilki taso Hamrah ta fad'a mata wani abu daga zaman ta agidansu sam Hamrah taki fad'a sai kuka kawai takeyi,  sosai suka shiga damuwa domin sun tabbatar akwai abinda yake damunta kawai ba ta fad'a bane. Ganin zasu takurata yasa suka kyaleta. 



Sosai suke nuna mata gata,  shiri na musamman Aunty Bilki take mata,  haka ta d'auki waya ta kira Malan Bukar alokacin yana tare da Kubra,  bayan sun gama gaisawa tace ina Kubra take?  Malan yace gata nan bara na bata... Bayan sun gama gaisawa Aunty Bilki tace Kubra bugowa nayi na maki godiya akan rik'on da kike ma Hamrah,  gashi hatta da biki ma atare da Hibbah zaki had'asu,  gaskiya munji dad'i sosai,  domin kin rik'e amana. 



Baki Umma ta washe tana fad'in haba ai bakomai 'Dah na kowa ne,  Hibbah ta riga samun miji amma nace dole tajira Hamrah sai gashi itama ta samu dan nata ma yafi na Hibbah kud'i,  ai ni jindad'i ne ace na aurar dasu baki d'aya. 



Aunty Bilki tace gaskiya kin kyauta,  sai dai ina neman wata alfarma guda d'aya. Kubra tace kifad'a mana ai ba wani abu. Aunty Bilki tace dama ina so abamu aron d'iyarki Hamrah nan da sati d'aya sai ta dawo dan Allah. 



Murmushi Kubra tayi tace haba Bilki,  yanzu saboda wannan abun ne sai kin rokeni,  ai mu duka Iyayenta ne,  dan haka bakomai Allah ya kaimu sati gudan. Aunty Bilki tace to mungode sosai sai munzo biki..... 



Tab'e baki tayi tana fad'in munafukan banza,  ai na d'auka ko taje ta fad'a masu gaskiyar abinda ya faru. Malan Bukar yace haba kema kinsan bazata fad'a ba. Tsaki tayi tace tayo sati biyu ma,  dad'in abun dama mun kusa rabuwa. Murmushi kawai Malan Bukar yayi yana kallonta. 



***


Washe gari tunda safe Umma ta nufi wajen bokanta,  tana zuwa babu b'ata lokaci yace mata aiki ne mai sauki,  haka  yayi wani zane kafin ya d'auko wata laya wadda tasha d'auri yace taje ta samu wajen da mutane basa amfani dashi ta tona rami sosai ta rufe layar,  babu wani wanda zaiyi mata magana akan lefe,  ko akan gidan da d'iyarta zata zauna,  ko ya gidan yake bazasu tab'a mata maganar ba,  haka zalika maganar lefe ma babu wanda zaiyi mata koda ba'a kawo ba har sai idan an tone layar. Kubra tace zama akawo sai dai bada wuri za'a kawoshi ba bana son surutu ne shiyasa nace arufe masu baki. Boka yace angama. 



Kud'i ta bashi masu yawa kafin ta tafi. Wajen wani fili taje inda bakowa ta tona rami ta rufe layar ta nufi gida..... Koda taje bata iske Hibbah agidan ba  kuma akulle yake saboda Malan Bukar ya fita. A waya ta kirata anan take fad'a mata Mufty take son gani kuma yace bazaizo yau ba yana wani aiki shine taji bazata iya jurewa ba taje ta ganshi. 



Salati Kubra tasa tana fad'in wane irin sabon salo ne haka Hibbah?  Kafin bikin ku ya matso baki wannan abun sai yanzu da aski yazo gaban goshi,  haba Hibbah bana son fa kiyi abinda zaisa ya fasa aurenki. Hibbah kamar zatayi kuka tace Allah Umma idan banganshi ba bazan iya cin abinci ba. Ajiyar zuciya Umma ta saki tana fad'in shikenan ina kika kai mani makullin? Hibbah tace yana gidan Laure. Umma tace to amma kidawo da wuri. 



****


Sosai ake gyara Hamrah,  duk da tana cikin damuwa amma tana kok'arin b'oyewa saboda bata so fushin mahaifinta ya tabbata akanta,  cikin kwana biyu tayi haske,  duk da akwai rama ajikinta amma fatar ta tayi kyau sai sheki takeyi,  domin gyaran da suke mata bana wasa bane,  bata samun lokacin kuka sai bayan ta kwanta,  domin kullum tana zullumin mijin da za'a aura mata. 


***

Bilal ma tunda ya sauka a masaukinsa baya da aikinyi sai tunanin Hamrah,  sosai yake kewarta,  gashi ko sallama basuyi ba,  gashi bata da waya bare yaji muryarta,  sosai ya rame saboda tunani,  aikin daya kaishi ma da kyar yakeyi,  duk lokacin da sukayi waya da Momynsa yana kok'arin b'oye damuwarsa. Paper ya samu ya rubuta ranakun auren Hamrah da Mahaifinsa,  kullum sai ya soke lamba d'aya,  a kullum kuma fad'uwar gabanshi k'aruwa takeyi domin yasan biki ya kusa. 


****


Ayau ne Hamrah ta dawo gida,  sosai Kubra ta tsorata da yanda tayi haske ga sheki tanayi,  duk da arame take amma tafi Hibbah kyan gani,  domin dama tafi ta kyau sosai,  duk gyaran da take ma Hibbah bata kai Hamrah sheki ba,  dan yanzu ita kanta Hibbar rama takeyi,  saboda damuwa data sameta ta matsanancin son Mufty,  wani lokacin idan ta dameshi yazo har haushi take bashi,  damma yana jin sonta aranshi yake d'aga mata. 



Umma har mamakin irin soyayyar da Hibbah take ma Mufty yanzu takeyi,  amma data tuna anyi mashi asirin mallaka sai hankalinta ya kwanta,  kuma tana ganin yanda shima yake kula da ita. 



Haka suka dage ana ta shirye shiryen biki,  yanzu ta rage takura ma Hamrah saboda bata zama sosai. Hatta da kayan d'akin da suka yima Hibbah a wajen mai kayan suka barsu saboda ba yanzu za'ayi jeren ba. Maganar lefe kuwa Mufty ya shirga masu k'arya yace jirgin daya d'auko kayan ya kone gaba d'aya,  amma zaiyi mata wasu kad'an da zata saka lokacin biki ya sake bada wasu kud'in a had'o mata wasu dan yafi son na waje. 



Tunda sukaji haka Hibbah tace bakomai ai,  Umma tace kawai yayi maki a kano bayan auren sai akawo na can. 'Bata fuska Hibbah tayi tana fad'in ni dai Umma kibarshi yayi abinda yace. Umma zatayi magana kawai Hbbah ta bar wajen tana k'unk'unai. 



Mamaki Umma tayi,  amma sai tabar abun akan Hibbar tafison na wajen,  hakan yasa ta bar maganar.... Kaya kala goma aka kawo ma Hamrah  d'inkakku sai kayan kwalliya da sauran na amfani  daga gidan su Abban Bilal,  Umma sai dariya take tana fad'in Alhaji ya iya tsiya,  duk kud'inshi amma 'yan kayan nan zai kawo mata,  kayan shafa ma a kirge suke,  gaskiya Hamrah baki da sa'a arayuwarki,  mai sa'a sai Hibbah wallahi. 



Ita dai Hamrah batace komai ba,  domin dama bata sa ran komai daga wajenshi ba domin ba son shi take ba,  babbar damuwarta rashin ganin Bilal.   Haka ta kwashi kayan da Umma ta watso mata a cikin jakarsu ta aje domin 'yan uwanta jibi zasu zo bata son suzo sugansu su fahimci wani abu. 



****


Yau biki ya kama saura kwana ukku,  sosai gida yacika da 'yan uwan Kubra dana Hamrah har ma da dangin Malan Bukar.... Muftahu kaya kala biyar sai takalma biyu da jakarsu,  sarka biyu inner wears guda hud'u,  kayan kwalliya acikin wata jaka da tarkacen sabulai maclean da brush sune abinda ya had'a ya zubosu a wata babbar jaka ya kawo ma Hibbah,  koda ta gansu ranta bai b'aci ba,  anan yace mata wad'an da zatayi amfani dasune kawai abiki tana zuwa ma zata bayar dasu domin nata sati mai zuwa zasu zo. Sosai tayi farin ciki. Koda ta kaima Kubra itama bataji komai ba,  saboda tasan zata samu wad'an da sukafi su, anan tace idan zaki bayar ki kawo mani kinga sai na k'ara. Hibbah tace dole ma ke zan ba Umma. 



Kayan abinci sosai Malan Bukar ya siyo da kud'in da Alhaji ya bashi na hidimar biki,  domin Kubra tace yasiyo kayan abinci masu yawa dan tafiso kowa yazo yasan ana bikin Hibbah. 



Sosai su Aunty Bilki sukayi mamakin rashin ganin lefen Hamrah,  sai dai sun kasa tambayar na Hibbah ma,  Aunty Bilki ce tayi k'arfin halin tambayar lefen Hamrah da kayan d'akinta. Kubra tace ai gaba d'ayansu kayan sawa kawai aka kawo wand'an da aka d'inka sauran suna can gidajensu saboda yanzu ba'a kawo lefe b'arayi ne suke sata.

Murmushi Kubra tayi tace Bilkisu kenan,  wai kina tunanin zamu cutar da Hamrah ne?  Aunty Bilki tace haba dai,  wallahi ba haka nake nufi ba,  kawai naga al'adace dangin Uwa sune suke zuwa jere shiyasa nayi magana. 



Kubra tace to ai tuni akayi mata jere kuma an saka mata komai saboda kinsan idan mata suka taru kowa da shawarar da zai bayar da haka har aje ayi mata b'arna,  shiyasa kawai mukayi da wuri,  dan haka ku aje kayan idan za'a kaita sai kutafi dasu ku k'ara gyara mata gidan. Aunty Bilki tace shikenan mungode.

Bayansu Hafsat tabi tana hararar Kubra a b'oye. Malan Bukar yana cikin abokanshi ya hango lokacin da aka fito da Hamrah,  murmushi ne ya bayyana a fuskarshi,  sosai yake jin farin ciki,  yaso ya samu keb'ewa da ita amma Kubra ta hana,  amma dole bayan biki yaje ya sameta,  babban farin cikinsa Allah ya canza al'amarinsa akanta,  ya bata abinda zuciyarta take so. 


Sosai kowa yayi mamakin irin gidan da aka kai Hamrah,  acikin unguwar Magajin Makera ne gidan yake,  sai dai ba gidan Iyayen Bilal bane,  gidan Bilal ne da Abbah ya gina mashi saboda aure,  tunda ya yanke hukuncin auren Bilal da Hamrah yaje yasa aka share gidan ya zuba kayan d'aki da komai. Ko Momy sai ana saura kwana biyu biki ya fad'a mata abinda ya yanke,  har sai da tayi kukan farin ciki,  sai dai yace kada ta fad'ama Bilal har sai ya dawo,  kud'i ya bata yace taje ta had'o kayan kitchen asa agidan,  haka taje ta had'o mata komai akayi mata jere,  lefe kuwa a kano sukaje suka had'o mata mai tsadar gaske suka jera mata a d'akinta. 



Aunty Bilki hada kukan murna ganin irin gidan da Hamrah zata zauna,  sai dai tana buk'atar taga mijin data aura. Ita kuwa Hamrah ko kad'an gidan bai burgeta ba,  saboda tasan zaman kunci zatayi,  gara akaita gidan bukka tare da masoyinta akan ta zauna anan. 



Hafsat ce taja Aunty Bilki dasu Hauwa k'anwar Aunty Bilki ta fad'a masu duk abinda taji. Sosai hankalinsu ya tashi,  tuni farin cikin da suke ciki ya b'ace. Hauwa ta fara masifa tana fad'in bazasu bar Hamrah agidan ba da ita zasu koma,  kafin wani lokaci mutane sun cika wajen kowa yana tambayar abinda ya faru. 


Nan aka fad'a masu komai,  Hajiya sai masifa takeyi tana fad'in ai dama jikina bai bani ba,  amma ya akayi Malan da hankalinsa ya yarda akayi wannan zaluncin haka,  koda yake nasan ba haka Kubra ta barshi ba,  amma kuyi hak'uri,  na tabbata ita kanta Kubra bata san a wane irin gida 'Diyarta take ciki ba,  hakan kad'ai ya isa ya tabbatar maku da Allah ne yayi ma Hamrah sakayya,  tunda an riga an d'aura auren kuyi hakuri,  insha Allahu auren nan sai yayi albarka. 



Aunty Bilki tace shikenan tunda kince haka,  kuma tunda ba'a gida d'aya zasu zauna ba bakomai Allah ya bata juriyar zama,  ita kuma Hibbah data aurar mata saurayi taje taci abinda zataci,  amma dole muje naga mijin Hamrah kafin mutafi. 



Hajiya tace wannan haka yake,  amma dan Allah kowa ya kwantar da hankalinshi,  kada muyi abinda zai zubar mana da girma,  idan rai ya b'aci hankali ake sawa yana nemoshi,  bara naje akwai 'yan uwan Alhajin a waje sai muce su kaimu wajen. 



Haka suka fita su ukku suka nufi gidan su Bilal.... Koda sukaje gidan cike yake da mutane,  wani d'akin da ba mutane aka kaisu kafin aka yima Momy magana,  tana jin haka ta kira Abbah ta fad'a mashi,  lokacin yana falonshi yace mata gashinan zuwa. 




Gaba d'ayansu fuskarsu cike take da farin ciki jin bayanin da Abbah yayi masu,  Aunty Bilki sai share hawaye takeyi tana murmushi. Hajiya tace Alhamdulillahi,  gaskiya Alhaji kai mutumin kirki ne,  hakika Malan Bukar ya had'u da kaidin mace,  Allah kad'ai yasan abinda tayi masa domin nasan da hankalinshi bazaiyi wannan abun ga marainiyar Allah ba,  mungode sosai Allah ya albarkaci auren su, kuma insha Allahu bazamu fad'a mata ba idan ya dawo zasu gani da idonsu. 




Dariya sukayi Momy tace akwai macen da zata zauna tare da ita kafin ya dawo dan haka kada ku damu. Hauwa tace mungode sosai. Haka sukayi masu sallama suka koma gidan Hamrah domin suyi mata sallama. 



Sosai sukayi mata nasiha suna k'ara jaddada mata akan ta rik'e Iyayen mijinta tamkar nata. Hamrah tayi mamaki ashe har yanzu Alhaji yana da Iyaye. Haka dai sukayi mata nasiha Aunty Bilki ta rubuta mata lambar waya a takarda tace idan an bata waya sai takira ta. 



Haka sukayi mata sallama sai kuka takeyi suka tafi. 'Yan uwan su Bilal ne suka jata zuwa ciki suna lallashinta.

Lokacin da suka koma sai suka iske gidan a hargitse,  suna shiga suka iske Laure da Kubra suna fad'a sai zagin rashin mutunci sukema juna,  inda Laure ta dage tana tona ma Kubra asiri,  anan take fad'in aikin banza,  kije kiga gidan da Hibbah zatayi rayuwa kila sai kin had'iyi zuciya kin mutu saboda takaici,  haka kawai kin zauna kina mana k'aryar irin gidan da za'a kai Hibbah,  hada cewa wai a G.R.A zasu zauna sai gashi ta k'are a tsakiyar Bagari. 



Nan fa hankalin Kubra ya tashi,  sai alokacin mutane suka fara fad'in irin yanayin gidan Hibbah. Da kyar mutane suka raba fad'an inda aka ja Laure aka fitar da ita. Tafi Aunty Bilki tayi tana fad'in Allah mungode maka,  wannan shi ake kira da kaikai koma kan mashekiya. 


Hauwa tace ko kuma ramin mugunta ba,  to gashi dai wad'an da suka gina sun fad'a ciki. Malan Bukar da yake gefe yana jin duk abubuwan da suke faruwa sosai jikinshi yayi sanyi,  domin yaji duk irin asirin da Kubra tayi mashi abakin Laure. 


Lokacin da yayi magana ne hankalin Kubra ya dawo kanshi,  sosai hankalinta ya tashi, ba dai yaji abinda Laure ta fad'a ba?  Tunawa tayi da abinda boka yace, duk ranar da mijinta yaji cewar itace tayi masa asiri shikenan asirin zai kare. 


Saurin juyawa tayi zata shige d'aki,  da sauri Hauwa tasha gabanta tana fad'in ashe dama auren Jari kikasa mijinki yayi ma marainiyar Allah? Dama zalunta kike ci bamu sani ba?  Amma kika b'oye,  sai gashi Allah ya karya alkadarinki,  idan baki sani ba irin gidan da kike ma d'iyarki fata shine Hamrah take ciki,  sannan idan kina tunanin kin rabata da saurayinta kin aurama d'iyarki to albishirinki,  sabon saurayinta da tayi shine dai Allah yasa aka d'aura dashi,  domin mahaifinsa shine wanda kuka ci bashi kukayima Hibbah kayan d'aki,  to Allah ya tona asirinki kafin ad'aura ya gano komai kuma ya fad'a ma Malan Bukar ya maida auren kan 'Danshi,  ina maki albishir da kije gidan Hamrah kiga aljannar duniya,  nan bada jimawa ba zasu d'aga Dubai domin acan mijinta yake kasuwancinsa kinga dole taje Dubai. 



Da sauri Kubra tazo shigewa d'aki saboda yanda jikinta yake rawa. Aunty Bilki ce ta cab'e zancan tana fad'in kitsaya na zazzage maki ta cikina,  wallahi kinji kunya,  kuma Allah bazai barki ba,  kinyi shirka sannan kin cutar da marainiya,  insha Allahu sai kin ga yanda alhakin Hamrah zai tambayeki macuciya. 



Wata mata ce tashigo tana fad'in yau kam Kubra zaki yarda da magana ta da nace kuyi bincike akan Muftahu kika ci mani mutunci. Da sauri Kubra ta kalli mai magana,  tabbas ta tuna lokacin da Lami take fad'a mata tayi bincike akan Muftahu amma tayi mata rashin mutunci. 



Lami tace nasan kin tuna,  idan baki sani ba bari na fad'a maki dalilin da yasa nace kiyi bincike. Muftahu da Allah yasa bai auri Hamrah ba ba wayon ku bane,  Allah yaga abinda ya gani shiyasa ya shirya hakan,  domin Hamrah baiwarsa ce tak'warai,  shiyasa ya had'a Muftahu da Hibbah har kukaje kukayi asirin rabashi da Hamrah. 



Idan baki sani ba bari kiji,  Muftahu matanshi biyu da yara goma,  yana zaune a Bisije a cikin gidanshi mai d'akuna k'anana guda hud'u,  biyu na matanshi d'aya nashi d'ayan na yaranshi. 


Duk da ya kasance d'an gayu amma bayada wata sana'a mai kwari,  domin kuwa wankin mota yakeyi sai k'arya cike da ciki,  da kuke ganinshi kamar baida shekaru to haka jikinsa yake,  sam baya son yin abota da sa'anninsa sai yara,  abokinsa d'aya da nasani wanda suke sa'anni kuma da bazarshi yake rawa,  duk wasu kaya da yake sawa yawanci na aro ne,  ko kuma idan abokinsa ya bashi,  haka zalika motar da yake hawa ta abokinsa ce,  sam baya zama da mata,  kafin matanshi biyu yayi wasu biyun,  yanzu haka yara hud'u iyayensu basa gidan,  ko gidan daya saka Hibbah haya ya kama itama abokinsa ne ya kama mashi,  ina mai tabbatar maki baza suyi shekara agidan ba zata koma cikin matanshi domin amshe sauran kud'inshi zaiyi. 


Nasan zakiyi mamakin ya akayi nasan haka ko?  To k'awata tasan komai nashi,  domin mijinta abokinsa ne,  sai dai baya abota dashi, haka zalika sunyi fad'a da abokinsa wanda yake taimaka mashi anan mijin k'awata yaji komai domin ya tona masa asiri,  dan haka yanzu kinji Waye Muftahu. 



Da gudu Kubra tashige d'aki tana kuka. Jiki a sanyaye Malan Bukar ya k'araso fuskarshi cike da hawaye,  kallonsu yayi yana fad'in ina mai neman afuwarku,  Allah ya gani bansan ya akayi na amince da abinda Kubra tace ba,  na cutar da Hamrah,  a k'asan zuciyata inajin abubuwan da nake mata bana kyautawa,  sai dai bani da ikon da zan ja da maganar Kubra,  na kasa rik'e amanar Marainiyar Allah,  na cutar da Mahaifiyarta gashi ta tafi ba tare dana nemi afuwar ta ba,  nagode ma Allah da yasa wannan auren bai tabbata ba,  nafi kowa farin cikin auren Hamrah da Bilal,  tun alokacin da Alhaji yazo mani da maganar,  duk da ina fargabar abinda Kubra zatace amma nayi murna domin nasan anriga an d'aura.



Aunty Bilki tace MaLan kada kace komai,  harga Allah banga laifinka ba,  domin kowa yasan yanda tasirin asiri yake,  sai dai ka dage da addu'a amma matarka bata cikin sahun *MATA JIGON AL'UMMA*,  bata kasance Uwa ta gari ba,  domin ta gurb'ata rayuwar 'Yarta,  sannan ta lalata imaninta,  sai dai kayi hakuri da ita tunda Allah yasa akwai zuri'a,  tunda dai Allah ya ceto rayuwar Hamrah daga lalacewa sai mungode ma Allah,  duk da mangriba tayi gaskiya bazamu kwana agidan nan ba,  nasan idan mukaje tasha zamu samu motar da zata kaimu gida,  dan haka ayau zamu koma. 



Malan Bukar yace baza'ayi haka ba,  tunda kunyi niyar kwana sai zuwa gobe dan Allah kuyi hak'uri. Hauwa tace Allah bazamu zauna ba gara mutafi. Hajiya tace tunda abun haka ne muje gidana ku kwana acan,  Malan sai kayi hak'uri kada kace zaka d'auki mataki cikin fushi,  koda wannan abun ka barta ya isheta,  ka daure kayi mata halarci kodan darajar d'iyarta. Malan Bukar yace shikenan nagode. 


Haka suka tattara kayansu Malan Bukar ya kira abokinsa ya d'aukesu zuwa gidan Hajiya. 



Kubra taci kuka har ta gode Allah,  sai yanzu take danasanin rashin bin shawarar Laure datace asake yima Malan wani asirin da yanzu ko wancan ya kare wannan bazai kare ba,  dole ta koma wajen boka.   To amma da gaske gidan Hibbah ba'a G.R.A yake ba?  Kada maganar Lami ta zama gaskiya fa,  badan kada tayi abun kunya ba gobe sai taje har gidan Hibbah domin ta tabbatar,  to kuma ai bata san gidan ba,  amma ai Yayarta Karima tasani dan dai daga gidan Hibba bata dawo nan ba ta wuce gida da yanzu agabanta za'ayi mata wannan tonon siririn,  zatace duk shawarar da take bata bata d'auka ba,  kenan itama tasan irin gidan da aka kai Hibbah amma da sukaje jere bata fad'a mata ba? Koda yake boka yace babu wanda zai iya mata maganar har akai Hibbah gidanta. 



Kuka tasa tana fad'in Allah ya isa tsakanina da ke Laure,  a bainar jama'a kin tona mani asiri,  shikenan tawa ta k'are nasan Malan saki na zaiyi,  wallahi Hamrah bazata kasance cikin jin dad'i ba,  dole na koma wajen boka ya rabasu,  ya sa mata ciwon hauka tabi duniya. Haka tayita sambatu ita kad'ai ad'aki,  mutanan unguwa dama tuni sun watse da sauran 'yan uwanta dan dama ba wani shiri suke ba.

Ango Muftahu ne yashigo hannunshi d'auke da leda ya rufe gida domin ko rakiyar abokai be samu ba yace masu ya yafe. A kwance ya iske Hibbah ta fara bacci dan takirashi har tagaji. Murmushi yayi yana fad'in amarya bakya laifi. Saurin tashi tayi tana fad'in sannu da zuwa har na fara bacci. 



Zama yayi yana fad'in kinsan hidimar biki yanda take. Hibbah tace na sani ai bakayi laifi ba. Dariya yayi yace kinyi wanka ko?  Kai ta d'aga tana murmushi. Fita yayi yayo wanka kafin sukayi sallah sukaci abinci. 



Gabanshi sai fad'uwa yakeyi yana tunanin ya fad'ama Hibbah maganar matanshi ko kuma ya bari sai bayan sun gama cin amarcin su?  Kai ya girgiza yana fad'in gara dai na bari zuwa gobe kada na b'ata daren farko na. 


Hannunshi Hibbah ta kamo tana fad'in Dear kamar kana da damuwa aranka ko?  Kai ya girgiza yace bakomai. Langwab'e kai tayi tana fad'in ni dai ka fad'a mani dan Allah. 


Mufty yace komenene idan na fad'a maki bazaki damu ba?  Hibbah tace wallahi bazaka tab'a laifi awajena ba,  dan haka ka fad'a mani komai. Gyara zama Mufty yayi ya fad'a mata labarinshi. Da mamaki sai yaga Hibbah ta rungumeshi tana fad'in saboda wannan abun ne kake damun kanka,  ko duka duniyace akan ka ina sonka,  bazan tab'a rabuwa da kai ba. 


Murmushi yayi ya rungumeta yana fad'in nagode Baby. 'Daukarta yayi ya fara cire mata kaya suka nufi saman gado........ 



Bayan da komai ya lafa Hibbah tana kwance tana kukan wuya yayin da Muftahu yakejin wani irin ciwon kai,  daya runtse ido fuskar Hamrah yake gani,  sake juyowa yayi ya kalli fuskar Hibbah yayi saurin kauda kai yana mamakin ya akayi Yayar Hamrah take kwance a gidanshi har suka aikata rayuwar aure da ita?  Hannu yasa yana dafe kanshi. 


A fusace ya tashi zaune yana dukan Hibbah yana fad'in mai ya kawoki nan ina Hamrah take?  A zabure Hibbah ta tashi tana zare ido.   Jin kalamanshi ya tuna mata da bayanin boka ( *Muftahu zai auri d'iyarki,  kuma soyayyar Hamrah zata fita daga zuciyarshi,  sai dai duk lokacin daya tara da Hibbah shikenan asirin zai kare sai an sake wani)*


*Maganar Ummanta ta tuna alokacin da tace kada ka damu boka,  yanzu afara jawo mana hankalinshi idan yaso idan ankusa bikin sai ayi mashi asirin mallaka*. 


Hannu ta d'ora akai tasa kuka,  tuni ta manta da wani ciwon da takeji. Saurin tashi Mufty yayi yana saka kayanshi yace ina sauraronki ki fad'a mani yanda akayi na aureki bayan Hamrah nake so. 


Cikin kuka Hibbah ta bashi labarin abinda ya faru. Nan take Mufty yaji wata irin tsanar Hibbah aranshi,  ya tsani mace mai zuwa wajen boka.


Da sauri Hibbah ta mik'e ganin yana saka kayanshi,  zafin dataji ne yasa tayi saurin komawa tana kuka,  rokonshi ta farayi akan yayi hak'uri amma ko kallonta beyi ba. Waje ya fita zuciyarsa tana masa kuna,  kai tsaye ruwa ya d'iba ya nufi bandaki. 


Sosai Hibba take kuka,  jitake kamar zuciyarta zata fito saboda damuwa ganin tasa masoyinta cikin tashin hankali. To kodai asirin mallakar beyi amfani ajikinsa ba? Boka yace koya gano itace ta rabashi da Hamrah bazai iya komai ba domin bazai iya rabuwa da ita ba. Kwanciya tayi tana fad'in Umma kin cuceni gashinan ya wulakantani,  kowace mace adaren farkonta anaji da ita amma ban samu ahakan ba. 


Koda Mufty ya fito falo yayi kwanciyarsa,  sosai yake cike da damuwa akan abinda sukayi mashi,  tabbas yasan bai tab'a son wata mace aduniya kamar Hamrah ba,  Allah ya jarabceshi da sonta gashi yanzu an rabasu kuma tayi aure,  hannu yasa ya dafe kanshi yana fad'in kodai alhakin matan dana zalunta ne yake tambaya ta?  Tabbas Hamrah bata da hakkina hakan yasa Allah ya tseratar da ita daga salon yaudarata. 



Juyi yayi yana aina kalar rashin mutuncin da zaiyima Hibbah,  dole ma subar gidan nan ta koma gidanshi,  gara ya amshe kud'in hayarshi yayi wani abun dasu da dai ya zauna da wannan maci amanar,  kuma badan bakin mutane ba wallahi gobe zata nufi gidansu,  amma zai kyaleta saita koyi darasi agaidanshi kafin ya korata. Haka yayi ta zancem zuci har bacci ya d'aukeshi.

Hamrah kuwa ganin har dare yayi miji baizo ba sai hankalinta ya kwanta,  matar da zata kwana da itace ta fad'a mata sai nan da sati d'aya mijinta zaizo,  dan haka tare zasu kasance ta saki jikinta. Jin haka yasa Hamrah taji dad'i har taci abinci kad'an. 




Kubra kuwa ganin har Malan Bukar ya rufe gida ya shige d'akinsa bai nemeta ba yasa ta saki jikinta domin tasan da zai koreta da bazai rufe gida ba,  tashi tayi ta nufi d'akinshi. 



Tana zuwa ta zube k'asa tana kuka tana bashi hak'uri. Murmushi Malan Bukar yayi yace bakomai yawuce domin baki cuceni ba,  idan akwai wacce zaki ba hakuri to Hibbah ce,  domin ita kika cuta. Kubra tace nagode kuma insha Allahu bazaka sake jina da wani abu ba. Malan Bukar yace tashi kije. 



Tana fitowa ta saki murmushi tace tunda baka sakeni ba ai da sauki,  dole na koma wajen boka bayan sati d'aya komai yayi sauki. 'Daki tashige tana tunanin Hibbah.

Washe gari da kyar Hibbah tayi wanka,  alokacin komai kama da Mufty bata gani ba,  bayan da idar da sallah kafin gari ya waye taji k'arar mota. Mufty ne yashigo fuskarshi a d'aure ya kalleta yace kisa kayanki zamu bar gidan nan yanzu,  maza zasu shigo su kwashe kayanki. 


Murmushin farin ciki ne ya bayyana agareta jin abinda yace,  aranta tace nasan dole aikin boka yaci,  gashi ma zai canza mani gida,  Allah yasa wanda yafi wannan kyau ne. 


Haka aka kammala kwashe kayan tass alokacin gari yayi haske sai dai mutane basu fito sosai ba,  a motar kayan suka shiga gaba ita dashi suka nufi asalin gidanshi. 



Mamaki ne ya kamata ganin gidan da zasu shiga,  kallonta yayi yana fadin nan ne asalin muhallina,  idan zaki iya zama kishigo,  idan bazaki iyaba amaidake gidanku. Da sauri ta fito tana fadin koma ina ne zan zauna indai tare da kaine. Tsaki yayi ya fito. 



Tsakar gidan cike take da yara kowa yana hidimarshi,  matan gidan da yake sun san da zuwanta babu wacce ta kalleta,  haka ta rakub'e Mufty yashiga ya duba iya kayan da zasu shiga d'akin. 


Daga gado sai katifa kawai suka shiga,  kayan kitchen akasan gado aka sasu sauran shirgin aka ajesu gefen gado. Kallonta yayi yana fad'in bani da wajen aje kujeru sai kisan yanda zakiyi dasu. Hibbah tace ko amaidasu gida?  Mufty yace gidan uban wa?  Kud'in hayar dana kama maki wazai biyani da kud'in bikin dana kashe?  Murmushi tayi tace shikenan asayar dasu sai abaka kud'in. Tsaki yayi ya fita ita kuma tashige d'akin tana hawaye.

Bayan kwana biyu. 


Acikin kwana biyu kad'ai sai da Hibbah ta canza,  domin har an raba girki da ita,  ko kallonta Mufty baya sonyi domin irin tsanar da yayima Hamrah haka yakeji a akan Hibbah kuma abinda boka dama yace masu kenan,  tunda ya kwanta da ita daren farko bai sake kwanciya da ita ba,  koya shiga d'akin a saman gado yake kwanciya ita ta kwana k'asa. 


Matan gidan basa sake mata fuska dan ganinta suke kamar zatayi rainin wayau. Itace suka fara bama girki na kwana biyu. Sosai take wahala saboda bata saba ba,  idan tashiga d'aki ita kadai tayi ta kuka.

Zaune Abban Bilal yake afalo shida Momy,  k'annan Bilal ne da suka dawo hutu suke ta tambayar Momy ta barsu suje suga amaryar Yayansu. Abbah yace ai zakuje ku bari sai yadawo kuma bance kowa ya fad'a masa ba tunda saura kwana hud'u ya dawo. 


Wayar Momy ce tayi k'ara hakan yasa sukayi shiru,  tana dubawa tace d'an halak gashinan ma yana kira. Tana d'auka sai taji ba muryarshi bace. Bayan sun gaisa daga can aka fad'a masu Bilal baida lafiya. Da sauri Abbah y amshi wayar,  bayan ya gama jin komai yace abokin Bilal yaje ya nema masa ticket yadawo gida kawai. Sallama sukayi yana masa godiya. 



Momy cike da damuwa tace shiyasa nace maka afad'a masa,  nasan yanda Bilal yake son Hamrah dole yasa damuwa aranshi domin yasan zuwa yanzu ta zama mallakinka atunaninshi. Abbah yace kwantar da hankali zuwa yau ko gobe zai dawo komai zaizo k'arshe. Momy tace Allah yasa.

Zaune Kubra take agidan Yayarta Karima tana bata hak'uri akan abinda ya faru sannan tana rok'onta akan ta rakata gidan Hibbah. Murmushi yayarta tayi tace yanzu kinyarda da abinda nake fad'a maki ko?  Kubra tace nayarda ni dai ki kaini gidan. Tace shikenan tashi mutafi. 



Mamaki ne ya kamasu jin ance sun tashi daga gidan. Farin ciki ne ya kama Kubra tana fad'in nasani,  ni nasan Hibbata bazata zauna a irin wannan gidan ba wallahi,  bara nakirata ta fad'a mana inda suka koma. 



Jiki a sanyaye take kallon Yayarta hawaye suna zubar mata. Tambayarta tayi lafiya?  Kubra tace wai ashe gidan matanshi ya maidata. Yayarta tace ta fad'a maki sunan unguwar?  Kai ta d'aga mata. Tace ai sai mutafi ko. Adedeta suka hau Kubra sai kuka take yayarta tana lallashinta saboda ta bata tausayi.... Bata k'ara sarewa ba sai da aka ajesu a k'ofar gidan saboda baida wuyar kwatance. 


Haka suka shiga gidan Kubra tana hawaye. Yayar ce kawai tayi sallama,  matan gidan suka amsa,  kafin suyi magana Hibbah tafito da gudu ta rungume Kubra. Tab'e baki matan gidan sukayi suka cigaba da aikinsu. 



Zaune suke ad'aki Kubra banda kuka babu abinda takeyi jin labarin da Hibbah ta basu,  Yayarta sai da ta zubar masu da kwalla saboda tausayi,  duk da tasan alhaki ne yake tambayarsu amma taji tausayinsu. 



Kubra tace amma ya akayi wancan asirin bai kamashi ba?  Doketa Yayarta tayi tana fad'in ashe bazakiyi hankali ba Kubra?  Lallai zuciyarki amace take,  to bari kiji idan har maganar boka zakiyi zan tafi,  dama rakoki kikace nayi kuma nayi. Kubra tace haba Yaya yanzu bazaki tausaya ma Hibbah ba,  gaskiya bazan tafi na barta agidan nan ba,  gara ta hak'ura dashi mu koma gida Allah ya kawo mata wani. 



Tsalle Hibbah tayi tana fad'in ashe kuwa zaki kasheni Umma,  ai bazan iya rayuwa babu Mufty ba,  wallahi ko acikin daji muke ina sonshi haka,  kuma ma banga abinda yayi ba,  ai nan ma gidanshi ne,  shikenan sai ahanashi ya maidoni,  kujeru kuma ai nawa ne,  dan na bashi ya siyar ba wani abu bane. 


Rike baki Kubra tayi tana kallon Hibba,  tashi tayi tana fad'in wallahi asiri ya maki,  ya mallakeki saboda idan kin gano asalinshi kada ki rabu dashi,  dole naje a karya wannan asiri. Murmushi Yayar  tayi tana fad'in Allah ya kyauta. 



Haka sukasha dirama ita da Hibbah akan sai sun tafi tare ita kuma Hibbah tace babu inda zataje. Da kyar Yayar Kubra ta jata suka bar gidan sai kuka takeyi. 


***


Kwance Bilal yake a saman gadon Momy duk ya rame,  Momy tana gefe tana mashi sannu Abbah ya shigo. Murmushi yayi yana kallonta. Gyara kwanciya Bilal yayi yana fad'in Abbah sannu da zuwa. 


Abbah yace kai zanyima sannu ai,  nida nake jiran kadawo nayi maka albishir nayi maka aure sai kuma ka kwanta ciwo. A zabure Bilal ya tashi yana fad'in wacece ka aura mani dan Allah?  Abbah yace gani nayi zanyi aure shiyasa nace a had'a da naka ayi mana tare,  wata d'iyar abokina ce na samo maka,  dan haka sai katashi muje mu kaika kayi jinyarka acan,  kafin ka wuce kuma kashiga ka gaishe da amaryata Hamrah. 



Komawa yayi ya kwanta yana dafe kirji. Kallonshi Momy tayi tace haba ba sai ya gaishe da Hamrah ba,  tunda shima ango ne ai gara ya isa gidanshi. Abbah yace shikenan,  zuwa gobe idan kazo gaishe mu sai ku gaisa. 



Da kyar suka samu ya tashi yayi wanka ya canza kaya ad'akinshi kafin suka d'unguma zuwa gidanshi,  dama sunyi waya da Malan Bukar hakan yasa yazo suka tafi tare. . 



Hamrah tana kwance tayi kwalliya dan Matar da suke zaune Momy takira tace tasata tayi wanka tayi kwalliya,  sam Hamrah bata tambayeta dalilin hakan ba. 



Tunda suka shigo gaban Bilal yake fad'uwa,  da kyar yake d'aga kafarshi har suka shiga cikin falon. Da sallama suka shiga hakan yasa su Hamrah tashi suna amsawa,  tana d'ago kai taci karo da fuskar Bilal wacce take kallon k'asa. Gabanta ne ya faad'i data ganshi,  hakan ya tabbatar mata da sauran Iyayenshi ne,  kenan mijinta ne da matarshi?  Sulalewa tayi k'asa tare da duk'ar da kai. 


Jawo Bilal Abbah yayi ya zaunar dashi yana murmushi. Cikin sanyin murya Hamrah ta gaishe dasu. Da sauri Bilal ya d'ago kai domin jin muryar abar k'aunarsa. Cikin sauri ya mik'e harya manta da wani ciwo. 



Dariya suka sa gaba d'aya. Juyawa yayi yana kallon Abba da Momy. Kai suka d'aga mashi Abbah yana fad'in amaryarka ce. Ba Bilal ba hatta da Hamrah sai da tayi saurin tashi. 



Jawota Momy tayi tana fad'in abinda kikaji haka yake Hamrah,  tabbas da Bilal aka d'aura maki aure ba Mahaifinsa ba. Malan Bukar yace haka ne Hamrah....... 


Labarin abinda duk ya faru ya basu. Dukewa Hamrah tayi tana kuka mai tsuma zuciya,  ashe har haka Umma take kinta ita da Hibba?  Gashi kuma Allah ya saka mata. 



Zama Bilal yayi yana sakin murmushi sai kallon Hamrah yakeyi. Kasa daurewa yayi yatashi da sauri ya rungume Abbanshi. Dariya suka sa gaba d'aya. 



Malan Bukar yace Hamrah na zalunceki ki yafe mani bana cikin hankalina. Kai Hamrah ta girgiza tace Baba nayafe maka Allah ya yafe mana baki d'aya. 



Abbah yace to Bilal sai katashi muwuce asibiti ko. Dariya yayi yana fad'in Abbah na warke wallahi. Momy tace ja'iri,  sai ka godema Allah da yayi maka wannan kyautar,  kuma ga Hamrah nan amana domin yanzu itace d'iyata,  idan naji kayi mata wani abu saina sab'a maka. 



Bilal yace har abada Momy,  Visa ma zanyi mata ranar lahadi tare zamu wuce. Dariya sukasa Momy tana fad'in babu inda zaka kai mani yarinya. Bilal yace ai sati biyu kawai zamuyi zanyo parking ne na dawo gida nacigaba da aikina anan. Abbah yace dama ai saboda itane kayi gudun hijira,  tunda ka sameta ai shikenan. Bakomai Allah ya kaimu sai ku tafi taren. 



Momy tace Hamrah kiyi hakuri kinji,  duk abinda akayi maki kada kirike kowa arai sai Allah yafi saka maki,  gashi sunyi zalunci kuma ya koma kansu,  dan haka kicire komai aranki,  ki daukeni amatsayin Uwa kinji?  Kai Hamrah ta d'aga tana murmushi. 



Sallama sukayi masu suka tafi hada matar da take zaune a Hamrah. Suna dawowa Bilal ya zube k'asa yayi sujadar godiya ga Allah kafin ya rungume Hamrah yana hawayen farin ciki. Itama hawayen takeyi saboda tsabar farin ciki. Janta yayi suka shige ciki yana fad'in muje muyi wanka muyima Allah godiya. 



***

Murmushi Malan Bukar yayi alokacin daya gama jin abinda Kubra ta fad'a mashi. Cikin kuka tace dan Allah Malan kataimaka kasa araba auren nan,  wallahi zamu iya rasa Hibbah saboda tana cikin kunci. 



Malan Bukar yace Kubra kenan,  keda kikace mani Hibbah tace babu wanda zai rabata da mijinta,  to meye naki nashiga tsakanin su? Kubra tace asiri yayi mata ai. Malan Bukar yace irin wanda kukayi masa ba. Dan haka ni ba ruwana,  kika sake sakoni ciki ranki zai b'aci wallahi. Tashi yayi ya bata waje. 


Kuka ta fashe dashi tana fad'in Allah ya kaimu gobe ai ina da sauran kud'in biki wajen Boka zan koma dole ya karya asirin kuma ya sake bani na mallakar ka dana mallakar mijin Hibbah. 


***

 Zaune take agaban boka tana kuka bayan ta gama fad'a masa abinda yake faruwa. Wata irin dariya boka ya kwashe da ita yana fad'in ai dama na fad'a maki,  duk ranar da mijinta ya sadu da ita asirin farko zai kare,  kunzo na baku na biyu sai dai baiyi amfani ba,  domin shi baisha zob'on ba d'iyarki ce tasha. saboda aranar na tura aljanu sunje har gidan sunga abinda ya faru,  sam d'iyarki ta manta bata k'arasa zubar da zob'on ba ta shanye sauran hakan yasa abinda zai faru da mijinta ya koma kanta, kuma na fad'a maki wannan maganin baida makari. 


Magana akan mijinki kuma ni yanzu bazan iya masa komai ba,  domin ayanzu yafi dagewa akan baya,  tunda asirinki ya tonu ya dena bacci duka dare,  dan haka bazan iya masa komai ba. 



Cikin kuka Kubra tace yanzu babu yanda za'ayi araba auren Hibbah?  Dariya yayi yace idan kina son mutuwarta ko haukacewarta to zan iya raba aure,  sai dai daga ranar data rabu dashi zata haukace ko kuma ta mutu,  domin zuciyarta bazata iya jurar zama ba tare dashi ba. Kubra tace ka taimaka mani boka,  yanzu wulakanci yake mata,  abani maganin da zaisa yacigaba da sonta. 



Boka yace zan baki magani sai dai yana da sharad'i,  idan kika sab'a sharad'in zakiga abinda zai faru kuma bake kad'ai abun zai sama ba hada ni,  dan haka ki kula. Kubra tace wallahi zan kula. 



Wata k'aramar kwalba ya bata mai wani ruwa aciki yace atabbatar ta zuba mashi a ruwan sha kuma ya shanyeshi duka,  ina tabbatar maki bazai sake wulak'anta ta ba. Godiya Kubra tayi masa ta aje mashi kud'i,  anan ya sake jaddada  mata ta kiyaye. 



***

Tana zuwa gida ta iske Malan Bukar ya dawo,  kamar marar gaskiya tashiga,  azaune ta iskeshi yana binta da wani irin kallo,  murmushi ta saki tana fad'in dan Allah kayi hak'uri wallahi gidan Laure nashiga kuma muka tsaya muna fira. Malam Bukar yace ashe har kun shirya?  Kubra tace zuwa nayi na bata hak'uri ai. 



Malam Bukar yace yayi,  amma idan kika sake fita bada izini na ba abakin aurenki. Kubra tace amma dan Allah anjima zanje na duba Hibbah. Malan yace wallahi babu inda zakije,  ke nan da wata d'aya ban amince maki ki fita ba. Tashi yayi ya nufi waje. 



Hannu Kubra tasa tana dafe kirji tunawa da sharad'in boka. ( *Matuk'ar maganin ya wuce yau ba'ayi amfani dashi ba zakiga abinda zai sameki har ma dani,  kuma wallahi bazan kyaleki ba matuk'ar kika ja mana.*) 



Zubewa tayi tana kuka ga kuma sharad'in Malan,  saurin tashi tayi tana fad'in dole ma ayi amfani da maganin nan ayau. Waya taciro takira Hibbah. 



Tana d'auka tace kome kikeyi kibarshi kizo yanzu na anso maki magani kuma ayau akeso ayi amfani dashi. 'Bata fuska Hibbah tayi tace gaskiya Umma bazan fito yanzu ba,  ko sati banyi da aure ba ace na fita,  kuma Mufty ma bazai barni ba. 



Hankali atashe Kubra tace zanci Ubanki wallahi,  matuk'ar bakizo ba za.... Kashe wayar Hibbah tayi gaba d'aya,  sake kira tayi sai taji akashe. Salati tasa tana kuka. Da sauri ta tashi tana fad'in nasan Malan zai yafe mani gara naje na kai mata maganin kawai. 



Jakarta ta d'auka tayi waje,  garin saurin rufe gida dama bata zuge jakarba kawai kwalbar ta fad'o nan take ta fashe. Zaro ido tayi tana kallon abinda ya faru,  bata gama mamaki ba taji saukar wasu gigitattun maruka afuskarta. 



Wani uban ihu tasa tana dafe kunci. Malan Bukar da dawowarshi kenan dama ba nisa yayi ba ya tsaya yana kallonta. K'arasowa yayi shida wasu maza da suke zaune,  suna zuwa sukaga wani bakin hayaki yana tashi daga inda kwalbar ta fashe. 



Kubra kuwa ihu kawai take tana rufe fuska saboda har yanzu tana jin marukan afuskarta. Girgiza kai Malan Bukar yayi yaje ya kamata suka shige ciki. Sauran mutane kuwa mamakin abinda suka gani kawai sukeyi,  kuma abun mamakin suna ganin lokacin da hayakin ya gama had'uwa ya shige cikin gidan,  ai da sauri suka bar wajen. 



Kafin wani lokaci Kubra ta fita hayyacinta,  nan take hayakin yashige mata idanuwa ta saki k'ara tana rufe ido. Addu'a Malan Bukar ya fara tofa mata kafin ta fara natsuwa,  sai dai tana bud'e ido taga bata ganin komai. 



Kuka ta fara tana fad'in bana ganin komai Malan,  nashiga ukku na cuci kaina,  dama boka yace kada na bari kwalbar ta fashe,  yace ayi maganin yau gashi banyi kod'aya ba ya fashe. Tashi malan yayi yana fad'in nagode Allah da hakan ta faru dake,  kuma ki sani wallahi bazan zauna dake amakance ba,  hak'urin da zanyi maki d'aya ne zakici darajar Hibbah,  amma dole na sake aure wallahi domin banyi tsufan dazan zauna haka ba. Waya ya ciro yakira yayarta ya sheda mata abinda yake faruwa,  nan tace gata nan zuwa. 




Koda tazo tuni fuskar Kubra ta juye tayi wani irin baki. Nan ta zauna tana ta mata fad'a. Kubra tace Yaya wallahi nayi nadama,  na cuci kaina,  ban kasance Uwa ta gari ba,  haka zalika ban kasance cikin jerin *MATA JIGON AL'UMMA BA,* domin banyi wani abu da zai taimaki Al'umma ba,  babu wani hali mai kyau da wani zaiyi koyi dashi acikin halayena,  na cutar da d'iyata,  na jefata cikin masifar rayuwa gashi bansan ya zata kasance ba,  a kowane lokaci mijinta zai iya rabuwa da ita,  gashi bazata iya rayuwa babu shi ba. 



Tsaki Yayarta tayi tana fad'in kinga abinda nake fad'a maki ko Kubra,  yanzu me kika amfana dashi arayuwarki?  Gashinan kinyi biyu babu,  kin cutar da kanki,  kin cuci Hibbah,  ga hakkin Hamrah ina zakije dashi?  Hatta da Mahaifiyarta kin cutar da ita gashi bata duniyar,  wacce kikaso kisaka rayuwarta cikin tashin hankali gashinan ya koma akan d'iyarki wallahi Kubra kin cuci kanki,  kin gurb'ata rayuwarki abanza,  gashinan yanzu kin makance,  kuma ga aure Malan yace zaiyi. Kubra tace nayi nadama Yaya ki tayani addu'a Allah yasa iya kar abinda boka zai mani kenan. Yaya tace addu'a zaki dage babu abinda yafi k'arfinta. 



Haka ta kama Kubra ta kaita d'aki ta gyara mata gidan tayi abinci kafin ta aje mata komai tayi mata sallama cike da tausayin ta,  duk da ba uwarsu d'aya ba uba kawai suka had'a amma tana sonta,  dama itace take nuna mata wariya. 


****


A cikin kwana biyu Bilal da Hamrah sai da suka canza,  sun samu kwanciyar hankali har kiba sunyi, sosai suke faranta ma junansu rai,  duk dare suna wajen Momy acan suke fira kafin su dawo. Bilal ya saya mata sabuwar waya mai tsadar gaske had'e da sabon layi,  nan ta kira Aunty Bilki sukasha fira,  sosai sukaji dad'in jin tana cikin farin ciki,  kuma ta fad'a mata zasu tafi Dubai amma zasu fara zuwa su gaishesu. 



**


Rayuwar Hibbah ta zama tamkar baiwa agidan Mufty,  sam baya d'aga mata k'afa ko d'akinta baya shiga,  haka matanshi ma babu wacce take kulata,  aiki kuma dole tayishi ranar girkinta,  tuni ta lalace tayi baki kamar ba ita ba,  gashi saboda soyayya taki kunna wayarta dan kada Umma takira ta. 



Ranar data cika kwana takwas aranar tsautsayi yasa tayima Mufty laifi,  babu b'ata lokaci yayi mata saki ukku a jere kuma yace ya bata lokaci kad'an ta kwashe kayanta ta bar mashi gida. Ihu Hibbah tasa ta zube k'asa tana rokonshi yayi hak'uri,  ko kallonta beyi ba yawuce. 



Ganin haka yasa ta d'auki hijabinta da sauri tayi waje tana kuka,  aranta kuwa ta aiyana bazata kwashe kayan ba dole ta dawo dan bazata iya rayuwa babushi ba. 




Lokacin da taje gida Yayar Kubra ta iske tana gyaran gidan,  Malan Bukar yana d'aki yana karatu. Da ihu tashiga tana fad'in Umma kin cuceni,  gashi kinsa na cuci Hamrah mun rabata da saurayinta gashi ya aureni amma ban samu farin ciki ba,  yanzu ya rabu dani kuma wallahi bazan iya rayuwa babsu shi ba. 



Hawaye ne suka zubo ma Kubra jin abinda Hibbah tace,  dama tasan za'ayi haka. Tsawar da Yaya ta daka mata ne yasa tayi shiru,  cike da masifa take fad'in baki ganin halin da take ciki kikazo kina mata tsawa saman kai,  Kubra ba Uwarki bace saboda rashin tarbiya kike fad'a mata magana. 



Fitowa Malan Bukar yayi yana fad'in ai duk abinda ya samu shamuwa watan bakwai ne yaja mata,  wannan kad'ai ya ishe su iashara. Zubewa Hibbah tayi tana fad'in dan Allah Baba kaje ka bashi hak'uri wallahi mutuwa zanyi. 



Malan Bukar yace saki nawa ya maki?  Hibba tace 3 ne. Girgiza kai yayi yace kin tab'a ganin anyi kome a saki ukku?  Hibbah tace wallahi Baba bazan iya rayuwa babushi ba. Malan Bukar yace ga mahaifiyarki nan saiki fad'a mata,  idan yaso ta kaiki wajen bokan da kuka amso maganin mallaka ya cire maki abinda kikasha,  domin ta fad'a mana komai. 



Fita yayi ya bar masu gidan saboda zaije wajen matar da yake neman aurenta. Jawo Hibbah Kubra tayi tana kuka tace kiyi hakuri Hibbah,  Allah ya nuna mana ishara,  ashe Muftahu baisha zobon ba kece kika sha shiyasa maganin ya koma kanki. 


Dafe kirji Hibbah tayi jin abinda Kubra tace. Cikin kuka tace Umma yanzu babu yanda za'ayi akarya? Kubra tace kiran da nayi maki kenan aranar amma kika kashe wayar,  yanzu kinga yanda Allah ya maidani,  ayanzu nayi nadama inaso na gyara kuskurena,  kema ki nutsu mu koma ga Allah kozai yafe mana,  inaso kije gidan Hamrah ki bata hak'uri. 



Kuka Hibbah ta fashe dashi tana rik'e zuciyarta saboda wata irin soyayyar Mufty dataji ta sake shigarta. Tausayi suka ba Yaya hakan yasa ta zauna tana fad'in dole akai Hibbah wajen malamin addini,  k'arya boka yake yace maganinshi baida makari,  zamu dage da addu'u insha Allahu abinda yake fad'a bazai tabbata akanta ba. Kubra tace nagode sosai Yaya. 


****


Cikin sa'a Visar Hamrah ta fito,  nan fa suka fara shirin tafiya Dubai saboda wata biyu zasuyi kafin su dawo. A ranar Bilal yace ta shirya zasuje suyima su Baba sallama washe gari suje wajen su Aunty Bilki tunda sai jibi zasu tafi. Sam bataso zuwa gidan ba,  Baban ta ne kuma suna gaisawa kullum awaya,  amma dan kada Bilal ya fuskanci wani abu yasa ta amsa mashi ta tashi ta shirya. 


**

Kuka kawai Hamrah takeyi lokacin data gama jin labarin da Kubra take bata,  hatta da Bilal sai da kwalla ta cika masa ido,  ya jinjina rashin imani irin na Kubra,  tausayi da soyayyar Hamrah suka sake shigarshi. Malan Bukar kuwa jiyayi kamar ya nutse saboda kunya agaban surikinsa matarsa take tona asirin kanta. 



Gafarar Hamrah Kubra da Hibbah suka nema suna kuka. Hamrah tace Umma na yafe maku Allah ya baki lafiya,  dama zamu tafi Dubai ne jibi shine mukazo muyi maku sallama. Kubra tace ikon Allah,  zaku biya har k'asa mai tsarki ko?  Bilal yace insha Allahu tunda an kusa aikin hajjin. 



Kubra tace ina rokon alfarmar ki roko ma Hibbah sauki akan wannan lalura ta ta,  saboda yanzu kullum cikin kuka take akaita wajen mijinta,  ni ban damu na k'arashe rayuwata haka ba,  amma ina fatan Allah ya sauk'ak'a mata wanan masifa. 


Hamrah tace insha Allahu zan tayaku addu'a Umma kuma komai zai wuce. Haka Hamrah ta kawo kud'i masu yawa wanda take tarawa da sabulai da turaruka ta bama Kubra tace gobe kafin su wuce zata kawo ma Hibbah nata kyautar dama bata san tana gida ba. 



Bilal ma kyauta mai tsoka yayi masu haka sukayi masu sallama suka fita. Tun acikin mota Bilal ya rungume Hamrah yana saka mata albarka,  kallonta yake cike da so yace Baby na tabbas kina cikin jerin *MATA JIGON AL'UMMA*, Allah ya albarkaci rayuwarki ya saka maki da aljanna. Hamrah tace tare da kai abun alfahari na. 


***


Washe gari sukaje ma su Aunty Bikki sallama,  sosai sukaji dad'in zuwansu,  sunsha tarba mai kyau,  yini guda sukayi acan,  nan suka cikasu da abun arziki kafin suka tafi kamar kada su rabu. 



 Kwance Hibbah take hawaye suna zubowa daga idanuwanta,  sosai take son ganin Mufty amma Malan Bukar ya hana ta fita,  idan takirashi baya d'auka,  daga baya ma yayi blocking d'inta. 



Juyawa tayi ta kalli akwatin kayan da Hamrah ta kawo mata d'azu,  girgiza kai tayi tana tuna kalar rashin mutuncin da tayi mata yau gashi Allah ya d'agata har tana bata jace,  kuma zata bar k'asar abinda taso ace itace a wannan rayuwa,  tabbas Ummanta ta cuceta,  itama ta biyema son zuciya gashi ta barota. 



Muryar Umma taji tana kwala mata kira tazo ta rakata hanyar band'aki. Tashi tayi tana goge idonta ta fita. 


**

Washe gari su Hamrah suka d'aga zuwa Dubai cike da farin ciki,  sosai tayi kewar Momy,  domin yanzu itace amadadin mahaifiyarta,  tana kula da ita sosai,  sam yanzu bata kukan rashin Uwa sai dai tayi mata addu'a,  bata tab'a tunanin zata samu irin wannan rayuwar ba. 



**

Bayan sati biyu sosai Hibbah tayi baki ta rame,  alokacin tuni Malan Bukar ya tare da amaryarsa Hafsat wadda mijinta ya mutu ya barta da yara 3, kuma dukansu Malan Bukar ya amshe su yace 'Dah na kowa ne. 



Lafiya lau suke zaune da Kubra,  dan Hafsat bata da matsala,  kuma komai na gidan ita keyi bata nuna gazawa. Hibbah kuwa babu lafiya bare ta samu zarafin aiki,  gashi Malan Bukar ya kafa mata dokar rashin fita hakan ya hanata zuwa taga rabin ranta. 



Yau ta tashi ta kudurin dole taje ta ganshi koma mezai faru. Bayan tayi wanka Hafsat tana d'aki ta sulale bata sani ba. 




Har k'ofar gidanshi taje nan tayi sallama tashiga,  tana shiga ta iske matan gidan sunyi zugum kowa damuwa cike da fuskarshi. Zama tayi tana gaishe su nan suka amsa cike da sakin fuska. Bayan sun gama gaisawa ta tambayesu ko Muftahu yana nan. 



Uwargidan tace uhmm,  ke dai bari Hibbah,  inaji alhakinki ne ya kamashi,  tun bayan tafiyarki yaga baki kwashe kayan d'akinki ba kawai ya kawo dillali ya sayesu yace mana zaije kano ya saro kaya yana so ya bud'e shago. Tunda ya tafi sai da yayi kwana ukku bai dawo ba,  sai jiya aka kawo manashi duk an bubbugeshi. Wai ashe da yaje kano ya had'u da wata burdurwa anan yaso ya nuna halin nashi na yaudara ashe ta fishi iya iskanci,  haka tasa abokan iskancinta suka kamashi suka kwace mashi kud'i,  sai da yayi kwana biyu awajensu suna gana mashi azaba kafin suka wurgar dashi,  da kyar aka samu ya farfad'o anan aka tambayeshi inda yake shine yayi masu kwatance aka kawoshi jiya,  yanzu haka yana d'aki akwance sun karya mashi hannu da k'afa an mashi d'orin gida. 



Cike da damuwa Hibbah tashiga d'akin. Kuka tasa tana fad'in Allah ya saka maka Mufty,  dan Allah ka maido ni nazo nayi jinyarka. Hawaye ne suka zubo mashi na tausayin Hibbah,  da ace ba saki ukku yayi mata ba daya maidota,  domin ya gano irin son da take masa,  kuma gara ka zauna da wacce take sonka arayuwa akan wacce bata sonka. 



Kai ya girgiza yana fad'in kiyi hak'uri da abinda ya faru,  kiyafe mani na cutar dake,  amma babu sauran aure atsakanin mu,  Allah ya baki wani mijin mai sonki,  fatana ki tuba kidena bin bokaye. Hibbah tace wallahi na tuba Mufty amma bazan iya rayuwa babu kai ba...... Nan takwashe abinda tayi mashi ya koma kanta ta fad'a mashi. 



Sosai ya tsorata da irin mugun halin Ummanta,  kallonta yayi yace kinyi kuskure Hibbah,  kinga gashi mun rabu kuma kina d'auke da cutar sona gashi babu halin kome,  wannan kad'ai ya isheki ishara,  nima wallahi na tuba da halin yaudara,  domin yaudara ce ta kaini wajenki,  da banje da niyar yaudarar Hamrah ba bazamu had'u ba,  duk da ina matukar sonta amma Allah yaga zuciyata ba mutumin kirki bane shiyasa ya had'ani dake. Babu abinda zance illa idan kika ganta kice ta yafe mani tunda yanzu tayi mani nisa,  tashi kije kuma bana buk'atar sake ganinki anan tunda babu komai tsakanin mu. 



Hibbah tana kuka tafito daga gidan har jiri take gani,  da kyar ta isa gida. Tunda ta koma ta kwanta ta fara ciwo mai tsanani. Kubra kullum cikin kuka take domin abun Hibbah ya fara komawa kamar hauka,  bata da magana saita Mufty. Malan Bukar abun ya fara isarshi hakan yasa ya kaita wajen wani malami nan aka fara mata addu'a tana sha,  cikin ikon Allah ta fara samun sauki.

*Bayan wata d'aya*. 


Hibbah ce tashigo gida sanye da dogon hijabi hannunta d'auke da jakar islamiya ta dawo,  dan tunda ta warke ta koma islamiya.  Sallama tayi ta shigo tana fad'in Aunty nadawo sannu da aiki. Hafsat tace yauwa sannu Hibbah ya makarantar? Hibbah tace lafiya lau ina Umma fa?  Hafsat tace tana d'aki yanzu tashiga tayi alwalar magriba kinsanta da wuri take shiga saboda lazimi. 


Hibbah tace su Faty basu dawo daga islamiyar ba?  Hafsat tace suna hanya dai. Ciki ta shiga tana fad'in bara na cire hijabita nazo na kwashe tuwon to. Hafsat tace kibarshi ki huta. Hibbah tace a'a ai bangaji ba  bara nazo. Murmushi Hafsat tayi cike da tausayin Hibbah. 



Zama tayi tana fad'in Umma na dawo. Hannu tasa ta lalubo Hbbah tana fad'in sannu Hibbah Allah yayi maki albarka kinji,  ai d'azu munyi waya da Hamrah a wayar Auntynki tana tambayarki nace kinje makaranta tace zata kiraki anjima. Hibbah tace Hamrah taji dad'i tana can k'asar waje ita da mijinta. 



Kubra tace kema ina maki addu'a Allah ya kawo maki miji nagari mai rufin asiri ba dole sai mai kud'i ba. Hibbah tace aini yanzu ko mai acab'a na samu zan amince dashi,  nayi nadama sosai Umma,  na gane rayuwa ka tsaya a matsayinka shine yafi sai Allah ya taimakeka,  kuma hassada mugun ciwo ce. Kubra tace haka akeso,  Allah ya k'ara tsaremu daga sharrin zuciya,  nima zanso ace kin shiga cikin jerin matan da akema kirari  wato *MATA JIGON AL'UMMA.* Dariya Hibbah tayi tace amin Ummana,  Allah ya amshi addu'arki a kaina. Kubra tace amin Hibbah ta.
